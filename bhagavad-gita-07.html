सप्तमोऽध्यायः
Saptamo 'dhyāyaḥ · Adhyāya 7 · Jñānavijñānayoga
श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
मय्यासक्तमनाः पार्थ योगं युञ्जन्मदाश्रयः । असंशयं समग्रं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छृणु ॥ १ ॥
mayyāsaktamanāḥ pārtha yogaṃ yuñjanmadāśrayaḥ | asaṃśayaṃ samagraṃ māṃ yathā jñāsyasi tacchṛṇu || 1 ||
Padaccheda
मयि आसक्त-मनाः पार्थ योगम् युञ्जन् मद्-आश्रयः असंशयम् समग्रम् माम् यथा ज्ञास्यसि तत् शृणु
mayi āsakta-manāḥ pārtha yogam yuñjan mad-āśrayaḥ asaṃśayam samagram mām yathā jñāsyasi tat śṛṇu
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: With mind attached to me, O Pārtha, practising yoga with me as your refuge, hear how you will know me in full, without doubt.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘me’ is the supreme Lord, with the attributes about to be described. To practise yoga is to collect the mind. One who seeks some human end takes refuge in its means, a rite such as the Agnihotra, austerity or giving; this yogī leaves every other means and takes refuge in the Lord alone. ‘In full’: possessed of glory, strength, power, lordship and the other qualities.

ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः । यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते ॥ २ ॥
jñānaṃ te 'haṃ savijñānamidaṃ vakṣyāmyaśeṣataḥ | yajjñātvā neha bhūyo 'nyajjñātavyamavaśiṣyate || 2 ||
Padaccheda
ज्ञानम् ते अहम् स-विज्ञानम् इदम् वक्ष्यामि अशेषतः यत् ज्ञात्वा न इह भूयः अन्यत् ज्ञातव्यम् अवशिष्यते
jñānam te aham sa-vijñānam idam vakṣyāmi aśeṣataḥ yat jñātvā na iha bhūyaḥ anyat jñātavyam avaśiṣyate
Word by word
I shall declare to you in full this knowledge together with realization, having known which nothing further remains here to be known.

Note Śaṅkara: vijñāna is one's own experience of what is known. The knowledge is praised to turn the hearer towards it: one who knows the truth of the Lord becomes all-knowing.

मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु कश्चिद्यतति सिद्धये । यततामपि सिद्धानां कश्चिन्मां वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ॥ ३ ॥
manuṣyāṇāṃ sahasreṣu kaścidyatati siddhaye | yatatāmapi siddhānāṃ kaścinmāṃ vetti tattvataḥ || 3 ||
Padaccheda
मनुष्याणाम् सहस्रेषु कश्चित् यतति सिद्धये यतताम् अपि सिद्धानाम् कश्चित् माम् वेत्ति तत्त्वतः
manuṣyāṇām sahasreṣu kaścit yatati siddhaye yatatām api siddhānām kaścit mām vetti tattvataḥ
Word by word
Among thousands of men, some one strives for perfection; and even of those who strive, the perfected, some one knows me as I truly am.

Note Śaṅkara: those who strive for liberation are themselves called the perfected.

भूमिरापोऽनलो वायुः खं मनो बुद्धिरेव च । अहंकार इतीयं मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिरष्टधा ॥ ४ ॥
bhūmirāpo 'nalo vāyuḥ khaṃ mano buddhireva ca | ahaṃkāra itīyaṃ me bhinnā prakṛtiraṣṭadhā || 4 ||
Padaccheda
भूमिः आपः अनलः वायुः खम् मनः बुद्धिः एव च अहंकारः इति इयम् मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिः अष्टधा
bhūmiḥ āpaḥ analaḥ vāyuḥ kham manaḥ buddhiḥ eva ca ahaṃkāraḥ iti iyam me bhinnā prakṛtiḥ aṣṭadhā
Word by word
Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, understanding and the sense of ‘I’: thus my Prakṛti is divided eightfold.

Note Śaṅkara: since this is the eightfold division of prakṛti, earth and the rest are the subtle elements, not the gross ones. ‘Mind’ stands for its cause, the sense of ‘I’; ‘understanding’ for the cause of that, the principle called mahat; and ‘the sense of ‘I’’ for the unmanifest joined with ignorance, the root cause, as food mixed with poison is called poison. This Prakṛti is the Lord's own power of illusion.

अपरेयमितस्त्वन्यां प्रकृतिं विद्धि मे पराम् । जीवभूतां महाबाहो ययेदं धार्यते जगत् ॥ ५ ॥
apareyamitastvanyāṃ prakṛtiṃ viddhi me parām | jīvabhūtāṃ mahābāho yayedaṃ dhāryate jagat || 5 ||
Padaccheda
अपरा इयम् इतः तु अन्याम् प्रकृतिम् विद्धि मे पराम् जीव-भूताम् महा-बाहो यया इदम् धार्यते जगत्
aparā iyam itaḥ tu anyām prakṛtim viddhi me parām jīva-bhūtām mahā-bāho yayā idam dhāryate jagat
Word by word
This is the lower. But know my other, higher Prakṛti, O mighty-armed, which is the jīva, by which this world is sustained.

Note Śaṅkara: the lower is impure, a source of harm, and of the nature of bondage to the cycle of birth and death. The higher is pure and is the Lord's very Self: the knower of the field, the cause of the holding of life, which sustains the world by having entered into it.

एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय । अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयस्तथा ॥ ६ ॥
etadyonīni bhūtāni sarvāṇītyupadhāraya | ahaṃ kṛtsnasya jagataḥ prabhavaḥ pralayastathā || 6 ||
Padaccheda
एतद्-योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणि इति उपधारय अहम् कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयः तथा
etad-yonīni bhūtāni sarvāṇi iti upadhāraya aham kṛtsnasya jagataḥ prabhavaḥ pralayaḥ tathā
Word by word
Understand that all beings have these as their source. I am the origin of the whole world, and also its dissolution.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘these’ are the two Prakṛtis, lower and higher, the field and the knower of the field. Through these two the all-knowing Lord is the cause of the world.

मत्तः परतरं नान्यत्किंचिदस्ति धनंजय । मयि सर्वमिदं प्रोतं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव ॥ ७ ॥
mattaḥ parataraṃ nānyatkiṃcidasti dhanaṃjaya | mayi sarvamidaṃ protaṃ sūtre maṇigaṇā iva || 7 ||
Padaccheda
मत्तः परतरम् न अन्यत् किंचित् अस्ति धनंजय मयि सर्वम् इदम् प्रोतम् सूत्रे मणि-गणाः इव
mattaḥ parataram na anyat kiṃcit asti dhanaṃjaya mayi sarvam idam protam sūtre maṇi-gaṇāḥ iva
Word by word
There is nothing else higher than me, O Dhanaṃjaya. All this is strung on me, like rows of gems on a thread.

Note Śaṅkara: there is no other cause; the Lord alone is the cause of the world. All beings are woven on him like cloth on its long threads.

रसोऽहमप्सु कौन्तेय प्रभास्मि शशिसूर्ययोः । प्रणवः सर्ववेदेषु शब्दः खे पौरुषं नृषु ॥ ८ ॥
raso 'hamapsu kaunteya prabhāsmi śaśisūryayoḥ | praṇavaḥ sarvavedeṣu śabdaḥ khe pauruṣaṃ nṛṣu || 8 ||
Padaccheda
रसः अहम् अप्सु कौन्तेय प्रभा अस्मि शशि-सूर्ययोः प्रणवः सर्व-वेदेषु शब्दः खे पौरुषम् नृषु
rasaḥ aham apsu kaunteya prabhā asmi śaśi-sūryayoḥ praṇavaḥ sarva-vedeṣu śabdaḥ khe pauruṣam nṛṣu
Word by word
I am the taste in water, O son of Kuntī; I am the light of the moon and the sun, the syllable Om in all the Vedas, sound in space, and manhood in men.

Note Śaṅkara: this answers ‘in what form is all this strung on you?’ Taste is the essence of water; the waters are strung on the Lord as that essence, and so in each case. pauruṣa: that by which men are known as men.

पुण्यो गन्धः पृथिव्यां च तेजश्चास्मि विभावसौ । जीवनं सर्वभूतेषु तपश्चास्मि तपस्विषु ॥ ९ ॥
puṇyo gandhaḥ pṛthivyāṃ ca tejaścāsmi vibhāvasau | jīvanaṃ sarvabhūteṣu tapaścāsmi tapasviṣu || 9 ||
Padaccheda
पुण्यः गन्धः पृथिव्याम् च तेजः च अस्मि विभावसौ जीवनम् सर्व-भूतेषु तपः च अस्मि तपस्विषु
puṇyaḥ gandhaḥ pṛthivyām ca tejaḥ ca asmi vibhāvasau jīvanam sarva-bhūteṣu tapaḥ ca asmi tapasviṣu
Word by word
I am the sweet fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire, the life in all beings, and the austerity in ascetics.

Note Śaṅkara: the natural sweetness of smell in earth is mentioned to show the same of taste in water and the rest; foulness in them comes from contact with particular things, owing to the ignorance and demerit of living beings. Life is that by which all beings live.

बीजं मां सर्वभूतानां विद्धि पार्थ सनातनम् । बुद्धिर्बुद्धिमतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ॥ १० ॥
bījaṃ māṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ viddhi pārtha sanātanam | buddhirbuddhimatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham || 10 ||
Padaccheda
बीजम् माम् सर्व-भूतानाम् विद्धि पार्थ सनातनम् बुद्धिः बुद्धिमताम् अस्मि तेजः तेजस्विनाम् अहम्
bījam mām sarva-bhūtānām viddhi pārtha sanātanam buddhiḥ buddhimatām asmi tejaḥ tejasvinām aham
Word by word
Know me, O Pārtha, to be the eternal seed of all beings. I am the understanding of those who have understanding, and the boldness of the bold.

Note Śaṅkara: the seed is the cause of growth. buddhi is the mind's power of discernment; tejas here is boldness.

बलं बलवतां चाहं कामरागविवर्जितम् । धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ ॥ ११ ॥
balaṃ balavatāṃ cāhaṃ kāmarāgavivarjitam | dharmāviruddho bhūteṣu kāmo 'smi bharatarṣabha || 11 ||
Padaccheda
बलम् बलवताम् च अहम् काम-राग-विवर्जितम् धर्म-अविरुद्धः भूतेषु कामः अस्मि भरत-ऋषभ
balam balavatām ca aham kāma-rāga-vivarjitam dharma-aviruddhaḥ bhūteṣu kāmaḥ asmi bharata-ṛṣabha
Word by word
I am the strength of the strong, free from desire and attachment; and in beings I am the desire that is not opposed to dharma, O best of the Bharatas.

Note Śaṅkara: kāma is thirst for things not at hand, rāga fondness for things obtained. The strength meant serves only to maintain the body and the like; it is not the strength that causes thirst and attachment in worldly people. Desire not opposed to what scripture teaches is such as that for food and drink just to maintain the body.

ये चैव सात्त्विका भावा राजसास्तामसाश्च ये । मत्त एवेति तान्विद्धि न त्वहं तेषु ते मयि ॥ १२ ॥
ye caiva sāttvikā bhāvā rājasāstāmasāśca ye | matta eveti tānviddhi na tvahaṃ teṣu te mayi || 12 ||
Padaccheda
ये च एव सात्त्विकाः भावाः राजसाः तामसाः च ये मत्तः एव इति तान् विद्धि न तु अहम् तेषु ते मयि
ye ca eva sāttvikāḥ bhāvāḥ rājasāḥ tāmasāḥ ca ye mattaḥ eva iti tān viddhi na tu aham teṣu te mayi
Word by word
And whatever things are of sattva, of rajas and of tamas, know that they come from me alone. But I am not in them; they are in me.

Note Śaṅkara: the things that arise for living beings through their own actions. ‘I am not in them’: the Lord is not subject to them, as worldly beings are; they are subject to him.

त्रिभिर्गुणमयैर्भावैरेभिः सर्वमिदं जगत् । मोहितं नाभिजानाति मामेभ्यः परमव्ययम् ॥ १३ ॥
tribhirguṇamayairbhāvairebhiḥ sarvamidaṃ jagat | mohitaṃ nābhijānāti māmebhyaḥ paramavyayam || 13 ||
Padaccheda
त्रिभिः गुण-मयैः भावैः एभिः सर्वम् इदम् जगत् मोहितम् न अभिजानाति माम् एभ्यः परम् अव्ययम्
tribhiḥ guṇa-mayaiḥ bhāvaiḥ ebhiḥ sarvam idam jagat mohitam na abhijānāti mām ebhyaḥ param avyayam
Word by word
Deluded by these three kinds of things made of the guṇas, this whole world does not know me, who am beyond them and changeless.

Note Śaṅkara: the Lord shows his compassion that the world does not know him, though he is ever pure, awake and free, the Self of all beings. The things are modifications of the guṇas, such as attachment, aversion and delusion; the world is the mass of living beings. Changeless: free of birth and every other change of state.

दैवी ह्येषा गुणमयी मम माया दुरत्यया । मामेव ये प्रपद्यन्ते मायामेतां तरन्ति ते ॥ १४ ॥
daivī hyeṣā guṇamayī mama māyā duratyayā | māmeva ye prapadyante māyāmetāṃ taranti te || 14 ||
Padaccheda
दैवी हि एषा गुण-मयी मम माया दुरत्यया माम् एव ये प्रपद्यन्ते मायाम् एताम् तरन्ति ते
daivī hi eṣā guṇa-mayī mama māyā duratyayā mām eva ye prapadyante māyām etām taranti te
Word by word
For this divine illusion of mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to cross. Those who take refuge in me alone cross over this illusion.

Note Śaṅkara: divine: belonging to the god, the Lord Viṣṇu, as his own nature. Those who give up all other dharmas and take refuge with their whole being in the Lord alone, the master of the illusion and their own Self, cross it: they are freed from the bondage of the cycle of birth and death.

न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः । माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः ॥ १५ ॥
na māṃ duṣkṛtino mūḍhāḥ prapadyante narādhamāḥ | māyayāpahṛtajñānā āsuraṃ bhāvamāśritāḥ || 15 ||
Padaccheda
न माम् दुष्कृतिनः मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नर-अधमाः मायया अपहृत-ज्ञानाः आसुरम् भावम् आश्रिताः
na mām duṣkṛtinaḥ mūḍhāḥ prapadyante nara-adhamāḥ māyayā apahṛta-jñānāḥ āsuram bhāvam āśritāḥ
Word by word
The deluded evildoers, the lowest of men, do not take refuge in me; robbed of knowledge by illusion, they have resorted to the nature of demons.

Note Śaṅkara: this is why not all take refuge in him. ‘Me’ is the supreme Lord, Nārāyaṇa. The nature of demons is marked by cruelty, falsehood and the like.

चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन । आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ ॥ १६ ॥
caturvidhā bhajante māṃ janāḥ sukṛtino 'rjuna | ārto jijñāsurarthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha || 16 ||
Padaccheda
चतुर्विधाः भजन्ते माम् जनाः सुकृतिनः अर्जुन आर्तः जिज्ञासुः अर्थ-अर्थी ज्ञानी च भरत-ऋषभ
caturvidhāḥ bhajante mām janāḥ sukṛtinaḥ arjuna ārtaḥ jijñāsuḥ artha-arthī jñānī ca bharata-ṛṣabha
Word by word
Four kinds of people of good deeds worship me, O Arjuna: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the man of knowledge, O best of the Bharatas.

Note Śaṅkara: the distressed is one overcome by robbers, a tiger, disease or the like; the seeker of knowledge wishes to know the truth of the Lord; the man of knowledge knows the truth of Viṣṇu.

तेषां ज्ञानी नित्ययुक्त एकभक्तिर्विशिष्यते । प्रियो हि ज्ञानिनोऽत्यर्थमहं स च मम प्रियः ॥ १७ ॥
teṣāṃ jñānī nityayukta ekabhaktirviśiṣyate | priyo hi jñānino 'tyarthamahaṃ sa ca mama priyaḥ || 17 ||
Padaccheda
तेषाम् ज्ञानी नित्य-युक्तः एक-भक्तिः विशिष्यते प्रियः हि ज्ञानिनः अत्यर्थम् अहम् सः च मम प्रियः
teṣām jñānī nitya-yuktaḥ eka-bhaktiḥ viśiṣyate priyaḥ hi jñāninaḥ atyartham aham saḥ ca mama priyaḥ
Word by word
Of them the man of knowledge, ever collected and devoted to the One alone, excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the man of knowledge, and he is dear to me.

Note Śaṅkara: he is devoted to one alone because he sees no other to worship. The Lord is his Self, and it is well known that the Self is dear; and he, being the very Self of Vāsudeva, is exceedingly dear to him.

उदाराः सर्व एवैते ज्ञानी त्वात्मैव मे मतम् । आस्थितः स हि युक्तात्मा मामेवानुत्तमां गतिम् ॥ १८ ॥
udārāḥ sarva evaite jñānī tvātmaiva me matam | āsthitaḥ sa hi yuktātmā māmevānuttamāṃ gatim || 18 ||
Padaccheda
उदाराः सर्वे एव एते ज्ञानी तु आत्मा एव मे मतम् आस्थितः सः हि युक्त-आत्मा माम् एव अनुत्तमाम् गतिम्
udārāḥ sarve eva ete jñānī tu ātmā eva me matam āsthitaḥ saḥ hi yukta-ātmā mām eva anuttamām gatim
Word by word
All these are indeed noble; but the man of knowledge is my very Self: this is my view. For he, with mind collected, has set out for me alone, the unsurpassed goal.

Note Śaṅkara: the other three are also dear, for no devotee is not dear to Vāsudeva; the man of knowledge is exceedingly dear. He is not other than the Lord. With his mind collected in the thought ‘I myself am the Lord Vāsudeva, and no other’, he has set out to reach the Lord, the supreme Brahman.

बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते । वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः ॥ १९ ॥
bahūnāṃ janmanāmante jñānavānmāṃ prapadyate | vāsudevaḥ sarvamiti sa mahātmā sudurlabhaḥ || 19 ||
Padaccheda
बहूनाम् जन्मनाम् अन्ते ज्ञानवान् माम् प्रपद्यते वासुदेवः सर्वम् इति सः महात्मा सु-दुर्लभः
bahūnām janmanām ante jñānavān mām prapadyate vāsudevaḥ sarvam iti saḥ mahātmā su-durlabhaḥ
Word by word
At the end of many births the man of knowledge comes to me, knowing ‘Vāsudeva is all’. Such a great soul is very hard to find.

Note Śaṅkara: births in which the impressions that lead to knowledge were gathered. With knowledge ripened, he comes directly to Vāsudeva, the inward Self. None is his equal or superior; hence ‘among thousands of men’ (verse 3).

कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः । तं तं नियममास्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः स्वया ॥ २० ॥
kāmaistaistairhṛtajñānāḥ prapadyante 'nyadevatāḥ | taṃ taṃ niyamamāsthāya prakṛtyā niyatāḥ svayā || 20 ||
Padaccheda
कामैः तैः तैः हृत-ज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्ते अन्य-देवताः तम् तम् नियमम् आस्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः स्वया
kāmaiḥ taiḥ taiḥ hṛta-jñānāḥ prapadyante anya-devatāḥ tam tam niyamam āsthāya prakṛtyā niyatāḥ svayā
Word by word
Robbed of knowledge by this and that desire, they resort to other gods, following this or that observance, constrained by their own nature.

Note Śaṅkara: this is why they do not come to see that Vāsudeva, the Self, is all. The desires are for sons, cattle, heaven and the like. Other gods: other than Vāsudeva, the Self. Their nature is the particular impression gathered in earlier births.

यो यो यां यां तनुं भक्तः श्रद्धयार्चितुमिच्छति । तस्य तस्याचलां श्रद्धां तामेव विदधाम्यहम् ॥ २१ ॥
yo yo yāṃ yāṃ tanuṃ bhaktaḥ śraddhayārcitumicchati | tasya tasyācalāṃ śraddhāṃ tāmeva vidadhāmyaham || 21 ||
Padaccheda
यः यः याम् याम् तनुम् भक्तः श्रद्धया अर्चितुम् इच्छति तस्य तस्य अचलाम् श्रद्धाम् ताम् एव विदधामि अहम्
yaḥ yaḥ yām yām tanum bhaktaḥ śraddhayā arcitum icchati tasya tasya acalām śraddhām tām eva vidadhāmi aham
Word by word
Whichever devotee wishes to worship with faith whatever form, I make that very faith of his unwavering.

Note Śaṅkara: the devotee is one of those moved by desire, and the form is that of a god.

स तया श्रद्धया युक्तस्तस्या राधनमीहते । लभते च ततः कामान्मयैव विहितान्हि तान् ॥ २२ ॥
sa tayā śraddhayā yuktastasyā rādhanamīhate | labhate ca tataḥ kāmānmayaiva vihitānhi tān || 22 ||
Padaccheda
सः तया श्रद्धया युक्तः तस्याः राधनम् ईहते लभते च ततः कामान् मया एव विहितान् हि तान्
saḥ tayā śraddhayā yuktaḥ tasyāḥ rādhanam īhate labhate ca tataḥ kāmān mayā eva vihitān hi tān
Word by word
Endowed with that faith, he undertakes the worship of that form, and from it obtains his desires, for those are granted by me alone.

Note Śaṅkara: the Lord, all-knowing, grants them because he knows how the fruits of actions are apportioned; hence they are surely obtained. If the words are divided as hitān, ‘beneficial’, that must be taken figuratively, for desires are beneficial to no one.

अन्तवत्तु फलं तेषां तद्भवत्यल्पमेधसाम् । देवान्देवयजो यान्ति मद्भक्ता यान्ति मामपि ॥ २३ ॥
antavattu phalaṃ teṣāṃ tadbhavatyalpamedhasām | devāndevayajo yānti madbhaktā yānti māmapi || 23 ||
Padaccheda
अन्तवत् तु फलम् तेषाम् तत् भवति अल्प-मेधसाम् देवान् देव-यजः यान्ति मद्-भक्ताः यान्ति माम् अपि
antavat tu phalam teṣām tat bhavati alpa-medhasām devān deva-yajaḥ yānti mad-bhaktāḥ yānti mām api
Word by word
But that fruit of those men of little understanding has an end. Those who sacrifice to the gods go to the gods; but my devotees go to me.

Note Śaṅkara: though the effort is the same, they do not take refuge in the Lord for a fruit without end. The Lord shows his compassion: ‘Alas, how hard it is.’

अव्यक्तं व्यक्तिमापन्नं मन्यन्ते मामबुद्धयः । परं भावमजानन्तो ममाव्ययमनुत्तमम् ॥ २४ ॥
avyaktaṃ vyaktimāpannaṃ manyante māmabuddhayaḥ | paraṃ bhāvam ajānanto mamāvyayamanuttamam || 24 ||
Padaccheda
अव्यक्तम् व्यक्तिम् आपन्नम् मन्यन्ते माम् अबुद्धयः परम् भावम् अ-जानन्तः मम अव्ययम् अनुत्तमम्
avyaktam vyaktim āpannam manyante mām abuddhayaḥ param bhāvam a-jānantaḥ mama avyayam anuttamam
Word by word
The undiscerning think of me as the unmanifest that has come to manifestation, not knowing my higher nature, changeless and unsurpassed.

Note Śaṅkara: this is why they do not take refuge in him. They think he was unmanifest and has only now become manifest, though he is the ever-evident Lord. His higher nature is his being the supreme Self.

नाहं प्रकाशः सर्वस्य योगमायासमावृतः । मूढोऽयं नाभिजानाति लोको मामजमव्ययम् ॥ २५ ॥
nāhaṃ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yogamāyāsamāvṛtaḥ | mūḍho 'yaṃ nābhijānāti loko māmajamavyayam || 25 ||
Padaccheda
न अहम् प्रकाशः सर्वस्य योग-माया-समावृतः मूढः अयम् न अभिजानाति लोकः माम् अजम् अव्ययम्
na aham prakāśaḥ sarvasya yoga-māyā-samāvṛtaḥ mūḍhaḥ ayam na abhijānāti lokaḥ mām ajam avyayam
Word by word
I am not manifest to all, veiled as I am by the illusion that is the union of the guṇas. This deluded world does not know me, the unborn and changeless.

Note Śaṅkara: he is manifest only to some, his devotees. yoga is the joining together of the guṇas; that itself is the illusion, yogamāyā.

वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन । भविष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन ॥ २६ ॥
vedāhaṃ samatītāni vartamānāni cārjuna | bhaviṣyāṇi ca bhūtāni māṃ tu veda na kaścana || 26 ||
Padaccheda
वेद अहम् समतीतानि वर्तमानानि च अर्जुन भविष्याणि च भूतानि माम् तु वेद न कश्चन
veda aham samatītāni vartamānāni ca arjuna bhaviṣyāṇi ca bhūtāni mām tu veda na kaścana
Word by word
I know the beings that are past, that are present, and that are to come, O Arjuna; but no one knows me.

Note Śaṅkara: the illusion that veils him from the world is his own and does not obstruct the knowledge of the Lord, its master, just as a magician's illusion does not obstruct his. ‘No one’: except the one devotee who has taken refuge in him. It is because they do not know his truth that they do not worship him.

इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन द्वंद्वमोहेन भारत । सर्वभूतानि संमोहं सर्गे यान्ति परंतप ॥ २७ ॥
icchādveṣasamutthena dvaṃdvamohena bhārata | sarvabhūtāni saṃmohaṃ sarge yānti paraṃtapa || 27 ||
Padaccheda
इच्छा-द्वेष-समुत्थेन द्वंद्व-मोहेन भारत सर्व-भूतानि संमोहम् सर्गे यान्ति परंतप
icchā-dveṣa-samutthena dvaṃdva-mohena bhārata sarva-bhūtāni saṃmoham sarge yānti paraṃtapa
Word by word
By the delusion of the pairs of opposites that arises from desire and aversion, O Bhārata, all beings fall into delusion at birth, O scorcher of foes.

Note Śaṅkara: this is the obstacle that keeps beings from knowing him. Desire and aversion, opposed to each other like cold and heat and directed to pleasure and pain and their causes, are themselves the ‘pair’. When they arise they bring the understanding under their power and produce the delusion that blocks knowledge of the Self. One whose mind is ruled by them does not know rightly even outward things, much less the inward Self. All beings are born already under this delusion; so they do not know the Lord as their Self, and do not worship him as such.

येषां त्वन्तगतं पापं जनानां पुण्यकर्मणाम् । ते द्वंद्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता भजन्ते मां दृढव्रताः ॥ २८ ॥
yeṣāṃ tvantagataṃ pāpaṃ janānāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām | te dvaṃdvamohanirmuktā bhajante māṃ dṛḍhavratāḥ || 28 ||
Padaccheda
येषाम् तु अन्त-गतम् पापम् जनानाम् पुण्य-कर्मणाम् ते द्वंद्व-मोह-निर्मुक्ताः भजन्ते माम् दृढ-व्रताः
yeṣām tu anta-gatam pāpam janānām puṇya-karmaṇām te dvaṃdva-moha-nirmuktāḥ bhajante mām dṛḍha-vratāḥ
Word by word
But those people of meritorious deeds whose sin has come to an end, freed from the delusion of the pairs of opposites, worship me, firm in their vows.

Note Śaṅkara: meritorious deeds are the cause of purity of mind. They worship him as the supreme Self. Firm in their vows: sure in their knowledge that the highest truth is so and not otherwise, with the vow of renouncing everything.

जरामरणमोक्षाय मामाश्रित्य यतन्ति ये । ते ब्रह्म तद्विदुः कृत्स्नमध्यात्मं कर्म चाखिलम् ॥ २९ ॥
jarāmaraṇamokṣāya māmāśritya yatanti ye | te brahma tadviduḥ kṛtsnamadhyātmaṃ karma cākhilam || 29 ||
Padaccheda
जरा-मरण-मोक्षाय माम् आश्रित्य यतन्ति ये ते ब्रह्म तत् विदुः कृत्स्नम् अध्यात्मम् कर्म च अखिलम्
jarā-maraṇa-mokṣāya mām āśritya yatanti ye te brahma tat viduḥ kṛtsnam adhyātmam karma ca akhilam
Word by word
Those who strive for release from old age and death, having taken refuge in me, know that Brahman, the whole of what concerns the Self, and action in its entirety.

Note Śaṅkara: with their minds collected in the supreme Lord. adhyātma is the reality that concerns the inward Self. These terms are explained in the next adhyāya.

साधिभूताधिदैवं मां साधियज्ञं च ये विदुः । प्रयाणकालेऽपि च मां ते विदुर्युक्तचेतसः ॥ ३० ॥
sādhibhūtādhidaivaṃ māṃ sādhiyajñaṃ ca ye viduḥ | prayāṇakāle 'pi ca māṃ te viduryuktacetasaḥ || 30 ||
Padaccheda
स-अधिभूत-अधिदैवम् माम् स-अधियज्ञम् च ये विदुः प्रयाण-काले अपि च माम् ते विदुः युक्त-चेतसः
sa-adhibhūta-adhidaivam mām sa-adhiyajñam ca ye viduḥ prayāṇa-kāle api ca mām te viduḥ yukta-cetasaḥ
Word by word
And those who know me together with what concerns beings, what concerns the gods, and what concerns sacrifice, know me even at the time of departure, with minds collected.

Note Śaṅkara: the time of departure is the time of death.
