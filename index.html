ग्रन्थालयः

Vedic Space Granthālaya

Sanskrit texts, verse by verse

The Granthālaya is the library of Vedic Space: a place to read Sanskrit texts slowly, one verse at a time, with what is needed to understand each word.

Contents
Every verse is given in Devanāgarī and in IAST transliteration, with the padaccheda, a word-by-word meaning carrying the lemma and grammar of each word, and a plain English translation. Notes mark the places where a commentator's reading shapes the meaning. The library now holds 3 texts and 853 verses.
Sources
The Sanskrit texts come from the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (Oliver Hellwig), GRETIL and sanskritdocuments.org, and the grammatical analysis from the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit. Dictionary entries are those of Monier-Williams and Apte, from the Cologne Digital Sanskrit Dictionaries. Meanings and translations are Vedic Space's own; where a text has Śaṅkara's bhāṣya, they follow it. Each page names its sources and the people behind them.
Navigation
Choose a text from the catalogue below. On a page, three switches show or hide the IAST, the padaccheda and the word-by-word meaning. A word in the word-by-word section opens its dictionary entry. Focus mode shows one verse at a time; longer texts have a section list in the toolbar.
Intention
To keep the reader close to the Sanskrit itself: to read, to chant and to contemplate. The translations are plain and stay near the words of the text. The library grows as each text is prepared and reviewed.
