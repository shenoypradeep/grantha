षोडशोऽध्यायः
Ṣoḍaśo 'dhyāyaḥ · Adhyāya 16 · Daivāsurasampadvibhāgayoga
श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः । दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम् ॥ १ ॥
abhayaṃ sattvasaṃśuddhirjñānayogavyavasthitiḥ | dānaṃ damaśca yajñaśca svādhyāyastapa ārjavam || 1 ||
Padaccheda
अभयम् सत्त्व-संशुद्धिः ज्ञान-योग-व्यवस्थितिः दानम् दमः च यज्ञः च स्वाध्यायः तपः आर्जवम्
abhayam sattva-saṃśuddhiḥ jñāna-yoga-vyavasthitiḥ dānam damaḥ ca yajñaḥ ca svādhyāyaḥ tapaḥ ārjavam
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: Fearlessness, purity of mind, steadfastness in knowledge and yoga, giving, restraint of the senses, sacrifice, study of the Veda, austerity, uprightness;

Note Śaṅkara: the divine, demonic and fiendish natures of living beings were pointed to in the ninth chapter (9.12–13); this chapter sets them out, the divine to be taken up as leading to release, the others to be avoided. sattva is the inner organ; its purity is dealing with others without deceit, trickery or falsehood. Knowledge is understanding the Self and other things from scripture and teacher; yoga is making what is so understood one's own direct experience, by withdrawing the senses and by one-pointedness. This is the chief divine endowment, that of sattva. Giving is sharing food and the like as one is able. dama is the quieting of the outer organs. Sacrifice is that of the Veda, the Agnihotra and the rest, and that of the smṛti. Austerity is of the body and the rest, to be described (17.14–16).

अहिंसा सत्यमक्रोधस्त्यागः शान्तिरपैशुनम् । दया भूतेष्वलोलुप्त्वं मार्दवं ह्रीरचापलम् ॥ २ ॥
ahiṃsā satyamakrodhastyāgaḥ śāntir apaiśunam | dayā bhūteṣvaloluptvaṃ mārdavaṃ hrīracāpalam || 2 ||
Padaccheda
अहिंसा सत्यम् अक्रोधः त्यागः शान्तिः अपैशुनम् दया भूतेषु अलोलुप्त्वम् मार्दवम् ह्रीः अचापलम्
ahiṃsā satyam akrodhaḥ tyāgaḥ śāntiḥ apaiśunam dayā bhūteṣu aloluptvam mārdavam hrīḥ acāpalam
Word by word
non-injury, truthfulness, absence of anger, renunciation, calm, absence of slander, compassion towards beings, freedom from craving, gentleness, modesty, absence of fickleness;

Note Śaṅkara: truthfulness is saying what is so, without unpleasantness or falsehood. Absence of anger is stilling the anger that arises when one is abused or struck. tyāga is renunciation, since giving has been named already. Calm is the quieting of the inner organ. Slander is disclosing another's weak points to a third. Compassion is towards beings in distress. aloluptva is the senses remaining unmoved in the presence of their objects. acāpala is not moving speech, hands and feet without purpose.

तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता । भवन्ति संपदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत ॥ ३ ॥
tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucamadroho nātimānitā | bhavanti saṃpadaṃ daivīmabhijātasya bhārata || 3 ||
Padaccheda
तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचम् अद्रोहः न अतिमानिता भवन्ति संपदम् दैवीम् अभिजातस्य भारत
tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucam adrohaḥ na atimānitā bhavanti saṃpadam daivīm abhijātasya bhārata
Word by word
boldness, forbearance, firmness, purity, absence of malice, absence of excessive pride: these belong to one born for the divine endowment, O descendant of Bharata.

Note Śaṅkara: tejas is boldness, and not brightness of the skin. Forbearance is no inner disturbance arising when one is abused or struck; stilling a disturbance that has arisen was called absence of anger. Firmness is the state of the inner organ that holds up body and organs when they flag. Purity is twofold, outer and inner. Excessive pride is thinking oneself greatly worthy of honour. ‘Born for the divine endowment’: fit for the glory of the gods, with good in store.

दम्भो दर्पोऽतिमानश्च क्रोधः पारुष्यमेव च । अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थ संपदमासुरीम् ॥ ४ ॥
dambho darpo 'timānaśca krodhaḥ pāruṣyameva ca | ajñānaṃ cābhijātasya pārtha saṃpadamāsurīm || 4 ||
Padaccheda
दम्भः दर्पः अतिमानः च क्रोधः पारुष्यम् एव च अज्ञानम् च अभिजातस्य पार्थ संपदम् आसुरीम्
dambhaḥ darpaḥ atimānaḥ ca krodhaḥ pāruṣyam eva ca ajñānam ca abhijātasya pārtha saṃpadam āsurīm
Word by word
Pretence, arrogance, excessive pride, anger, harshness and ignorance belong to one born for the demonic endowment, O Pārtha.

Note Śaṅkara: pretence is making a show of dharma. Arrogance is the swelling that comes from learning, wealth, family and the like. Harshness is harsh speech, as calling a one-eyed man fine-eyed or an ugly man handsome. Ignorance is want of discernment, false notions about what is and is not to be done.

दैवी संपद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता । मा शुचः संपदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव ॥ ५ ॥
daivī saṃpadvimokṣāya nibandhāyāsurī matā | mā śucaḥ saṃpadaṃ daivīmabhijāto 'si pāṇḍava || 5 ||
Padaccheda
दैवी संपत् विमोक्षाय निबन्धाय आसुरी मता मा शुचः संपदम् दैवीम् अभिजातः असि पाण्डव
daivī saṃpat vimokṣāya nibandhāya āsurī matā mā śucaḥ saṃpadam daivīm abhijātaḥ asi pāṇḍava
Word by word
The divine endowment is held to be for release, the demonic for bondage. Do not grieve: you are born for the divine endowment, O son of Pāṇḍu.

Note Śaṅkara: release is from the bondage of the cycle of birth and death; nibandha is sure bondage; the fiendish endowment likewise. Seeing Arjuna wonder within himself which of the two he has, the Lord says: do not grieve.

द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन्दैव आसुर एव च । दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे शृणु ॥ ६ ॥
dvau bhūtasargau loke 'smindaiva āsura eva ca | daivo vistaraśaḥ prokta āsuraṃ pārtha me śṛṇu || 6 ||
Padaccheda
द्वौ भूत-सर्गौ लोके अस्मिन् दैवः आसुरः एव च दैवः विस्तरशः प्रोक्तः आसुरम् पार्थ मे शृणु
dvau bhūta-sargau loke asmin daivaḥ āsuraḥ eva ca daivaḥ vistaraśaḥ proktaḥ āsuram pārtha me śṛṇu
Word by word
There are two creations of beings in this world, the divine and the demonic. The divine has been described at length; hear from me, O Pārtha, of the demonic.

Note Śaṅkara: the beings are men; they themselves, as created with one or the other of the two endowments, are the two creations: ‘the offspring of Prajāpati were of two kinds, gods and demons’ (Bṛhadāraṇyaka 1.3.1). From here to the end of the chapter the demonic endowment is shown as a mark of living beings, for once it is seen plainly it can be avoided.

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च जना न विदुरासुराः । न शौचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते ॥ ७ ॥
pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca janā na vidurāsurāḥ | na śaucaṃ nāpi cācāro na satyaṃ teṣu vidyate || 7 ||
Padaccheda
प्रवृत्तिम् च निवृत्तिम् च जनाः न विदुः आसुराः न शौचम् न अपि च आचारः न सत्यम् तेषु विद्यते
pravṛttim ca nivṛttim ca janāḥ na viduḥ āsurāḥ na śaucam na api ca ācāraḥ na satyam teṣu vidyate
Word by word
Demonic men know neither what is to be done nor what is to be refrained from. Neither purity nor good conduct nor truth is found in them.

Note Śaṅkara: pravṛtti is engaging in what is to be done as a means to a human end; nivṛtti is turning away from what causes harm.

असत्यमप्रतिष्ठं ते जगदाहुरनीश्वरम् । अपरस्परसम्भूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम् ॥ ८ ॥
asatyamapratiṣṭhaṃ te jagadāhuranīśvaram | aparasparasambhūtaṃ kimanyatkāmahaitukam || 8 ||
Padaccheda
असत्यम् अप्रतिष्ठम् ते जगत् आहुः अनीश्वरम् अपरस्पर-सम्भूतम् किम् अन्यत् काम-हैतुकम्
asatyam apratiṣṭham te jagat āhuḥ anīśvaram aparaspara-sambhūtam kim anyat kāma-haitukam
Word by word
They say that the world is without truth, without foundation, without a Lord, born of the union of the one with the other, caused by desire: what else?

Note Śaṅkara: ‘as we are mostly false, so is this whole world’. It has no foundation in dharma and adharma, and no Lord who rules it with regard to them. It is born of the mutual union of man and woman impelled by desire; there is no other, unseen cause. This is the view of the Lokāyatikas.

एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः । प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः ॥ ९ ॥
etāṃ dṛṣṭimavaṣṭabhya naṣṭātmāno 'lpabuddhayaḥ | prabhavantyugrakarmāṇaḥ kṣayāya jagato 'hitāḥ || 9 ||
Padaccheda
एताम् दृष्टिम् अवष्टभ्य नष्ट-आत्मानः अल्पबुद्धयः प्रभवन्ति उग्र-कर्माणः क्षयाय जगतः अहिताः
etām dṛṣṭim avaṣṭabhya naṣṭa-ātmānaḥ alpabuddhayaḥ prabhavanti ugra-karmāṇaḥ kṣayāya jagataḥ ahitāḥ
Word by word
Holding to this view, lost to themselves, of small understanding and cruel deeds, they arise as enemies of the world, for its destruction.

Note Śaṅkara: naṣṭātman: their nature ruined, having lost the means to the other world. Their understanding is small because it has only sense objects for its concern.

काममाश्रित्य दुष्पूरं दम्भमानमदान्विताः । मोहाद्गृहीत्वासद्ग्राहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽशुचिव्रताः ॥ १० ॥
kāmamāśritya duṣpūraṃ dambhamānamadānvitāḥ | mohādgṛhītvāsadgrāhānpravartante 'śucivratāḥ || 10 ||
Padaccheda
कामम् आश्रित्य दुष्पूरम् दम्भ-मान-मद-अन्विताः मोहात् गृहीत्वा असत्-ग्राहान् प्रवर्तन्ते अशुचि-व्रताः
kāmam āśritya duṣpūram dambha-māna-mada-anvitāḥ mohāt gṛhītvā asat-grāhān pravartante aśuci-vratāḥ
Word by word
Resorting to desire that is hard to fill, full of pretence, pride and intoxication, taking up false notions through delusion, they act with impure vows.

Note Śaṅkara: asad-grāha is an evil resolve.

चिन्तामपरिमेयां च प्रलयान्तामुपाश्रिताः । कामोपभोगपरमा एतावदिति निश्चिताः ॥ ११ ॥
cintāmaparimeyāṃ ca pralayāntāmupāśritāḥ | kāmopabhogaparamā etāvaditi niścitāḥ || 11 ||
Padaccheda
चिन्ताम् अपरिमेयाम् च प्रलय-अन्ताम् उपाश्रिताः काम-उपभोग-परमाः एतावत् इति निश्चिताः
cintām aparimeyām ca pralaya-antām upāśritāḥ kāma-upabhoga-paramāḥ etāvat iti niścitāḥ
Word by word
Given over to measureless care that ends only with death, holding the enjoyment of desires highest, convinced that this is all;

Note Śaṅkara: kāma here is what is desired, the objects, sound and the rest. They are sure that their enjoyment is the highest human end.

आशापाशशतैर्बद्धाः कामक्रोधपरायणाः । ईहन्ते कामभोगार्थमन्यायेनार्थसंचयान् ॥ १२ ॥
āśāpāśaśatairbaddhāḥ kāmakrodhaparāyaṇāḥ | īhante kāmabhogārthamanyāyenārthasaṃcayān || 12 ||
Padaccheda
आशा-पाश-शतैः बद्धाः काम-क्रोध-परायणाः ईहन्ते काम-भोग-अर्थम् अन्यायेन अर्थ-संचयान्
āśā-pāśa-śataiḥ baddhāḥ kāma-krodha-parāyaṇāḥ īhante kāma-bhoga-artham anyāyena artha-saṃcayān
Word by word
bound by hundreds of snares of hope, given up to desire and anger, they strive by unjust means for hoards of wealth, for the enjoyment of desires.

Note Śaṅkara: for enjoyment, and not for dharma. Unjust means are taking what belongs to others and the like.

इदमद्य मया लब्धमिदं प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् । इदमस्तीदमपि मे भविष्यति पुनर्धनम् ॥ १३ ॥
idamadya mayā labdham idaṃ prāpsye manoratham | idamastīdamapi me bhaviṣyati punardhanam || 13 ||
Padaccheda
इदम् अद्य मया लब्धम् इदम् प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् इदम् अस्ति इदम् अपि मे भविष्यति पुनः धनम्
idam adya mayā labdham idam prāpsye manoratham idam asti idam api me bhaviṣyati punaḥ dhanam
Word by word
‘This has been gained by me today; this wish I shall obtain. This is mine, and this wealth also will be mine again.

Note Śaṅkara: verses 13–15 give their thoughts. ‘Again’: in the coming year; ‘and by it I shall be known as a rich man’.

असौ मया हतः शत्रुर्हनिष्ये चापरानपि । ईश्वरोऽहमहं भोगी सिद्धोऽहं बलवान्सुखी ॥ १४ ॥
asau mayā hataḥ śatrur haniṣye cāparānapi | īśvaro 'ham ahaṃ bhogī siddho 'haṃ balavānsukhī || 14 ||
Padaccheda
असौ मया हतः शत्रुः हनिष्ये च अपरान् अपि ईश्वरः अहम् अहम् भोगी सिद्धः अहम् बलवान् सुखी
asau mayā hataḥ śatruḥ haniṣye ca aparān api īśvaraḥ aham aham bhogī siddhaḥ aham balavān sukhī
Word by word
That enemy has been slain by me, and I shall slay others also. I am the lord, I am the enjoyer; I am successful, strong and happy.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘what can these wretches do? No one is my equal.’ siddha: blessed with sons and grandsons.

आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया । यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः ॥ १५ ॥
āḍhyo 'bhijanavānasmi ko 'nyo 'sti sadṛśo mayā | yakṣye dāsyāmi modiṣya ityajñānavimohitāḥ || 15 ||
Padaccheda
आढ्यः अभिजनवान् अस्मि कः अन्यः अस्ति सदृशः मया यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्ये इति अज्ञान-विमोहिताः
āḍhyaḥ abhijanavān asmi kaḥ anyaḥ asti sadṛśaḥ mayā yakṣye dāsyāmi modiṣye iti ajñāna-vimohitāḥ
Word by word
I am rich and of noble birth; who else is equal to me? I shall sacrifice, I shall give, I shall rejoice’: thus, deluded by ignorance,

Note Śaṅkara: of noble birth: with Vedic learning in the family for seven generations. ‘I shall sacrifice’: and outdo others in that too; ‘I shall give’: to actors and the like.

अनेकचित्तविभ्रान्ता मोहजालसमावृताः । प्रसक्ताः कामभोगेषु पतन्ति नरकेऽशुचौ ॥ १६ ॥
anekacittavibhrāntā mohajālasamāvṛtāḥ | prasaktāḥ kāmabhogeṣu patanti narake 'śucau || 16 ||
Padaccheda
अनेक-चित्त-विभ्रान्ताः मोह-जाल-समावृताः प्रसक्ताः काम-भोगेषु पतन्ति नरके अशुचौ
aneka-citta-vibhrāntāḥ moha-jāla-samāvṛtāḥ prasaktāḥ kāma-bhogeṣu patanti narake aśucau
Word by word
led astray by many thoughts, wrapped in the net of delusion, addicted to the enjoyment of desires, they fall into a foul hell.

Note Śaṅkara: delusion, want of discernment, is a net because it covers. Hell is Vaitaraṇī and the like.

आत्मसंभाविताः स्तब्धा धनमानमदान्विताः । यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम् ॥ १७ ॥
ātmasaṃbhāvitāḥ stabdhā dhanamānamadānvitāḥ | yajante nāmayajñaiste dambhenāvidhipūrvakam || 17 ||
Padaccheda
आत्म-संभाविताः स्तब्धाः धन-मान-मद-अन्विताः यजन्ते नाम-यज्ञैः ते दम्भेन अविधि-पूर्वकम्
ātma-saṃbhāvitāḥ stabdhāḥ dhana-māna-mada-anvitāḥ yajante nāma-yajñaiḥ te dambhena avidhi-pūrvakam
Word by word
Thinking highly of themselves, unbending, full of the pride and intoxication of wealth, they sacrifice with sacrifices in name only, with pretence, not according to rule.

Note Śaṅkara: held in esteem by themselves, and not by the good. ‘Not according to rule’: without the parts and the procedure that the rule lays down.

अहंकारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं च संश्रिताः । मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः ॥ १८ ॥
ahaṃkāraṃ balaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca saṃśritāḥ | māmātmaparadeheṣu pradviṣanto 'bhyasūyakāḥ || 18 ||
Padaccheda
अहंकारम् बलम् दर्पम् कामम् क्रोधम् च संश्रिताः माम् आत्म-पर-देहेषु प्रद्विषन्तः अभ्यसूयकाः
ahaṃkāram balam darpam kāmam krodham ca saṃśritāḥ mām ātma-para-deheṣu pradviṣantaḥ abhyasūyakāḥ
Word by word
Given over to the sense of ‘I’, force, arrogance, desire and anger, these envious men hate me in their own bodies and in those of others.

Note Śaṅkara: the sense of ‘I’ is thinking oneself distinguished by qualities, real and unreal, superimposed on oneself; it is called ignorance and is the root of all faults. Force is that used to overpower others, joined with desire and passion. Arrogance is that fault at whose rising one oversteps dharma. ‘Me’ is the Lord, the witness of thought and action in their own and others' bodies; hating him is transgressing his command. They cannot bear the virtues of those on the right path.

तानहं द्विषतः क्रूरान्संसारेषु नराधमान् । क्षिपाम्यजस्रमशुभानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु ॥ १९ ॥
tānahaṃ dviṣataḥ krūrānsaṃsāreṣu narādhamān | kṣipāmyajasramaśubhānāsurīṣveva yoniṣu || 19 ||
Padaccheda
तान् अहम् द्विषतः क्रूरान् संसारेषु नर-अधमान् क्षिपामि अजस्रम् अशुभान् आसुरीषु एव योनिषु
tān aham dviṣataḥ krūrān saṃsāreṣu nara-adhamān kṣipāmi ajasram aśubhān āsurīṣu eva yoniṣu
Word by word
Those cruel haters, the lowest of men, evildoers, I cast ceaselessly, in the rounds of birth and death, into demonic wombs only.

Note Śaṅkara: they oppose the right path and hate the good and the Lord. Demonic wombs are those given mostly to cruel deeds, of tigers, lions and the like.

आसुरीं योनिमापन्ना मूढा जन्मनि जन्मनि । मामप्राप्यैव कौन्तेय ततो यान्त्यधमां गतिम् ॥ २० ॥
āsurīṃ yonimāpannā mūḍhā janmani janmani | mām aprāpyaiva kaunteya tato yāntyadhamāṃ gatim || 20 ||
Padaccheda
आसुरीम् योनिम् आपन्नाः मूढाः जन्मनि जन्मनि माम् अप्राप्य एव कौन्तेय ततः यान्ति अधमाम् गतिम्
āsurīm yonim āpannāḥ mūḍhāḥ janmani janmani mām aprāpya eva kaunteya tataḥ yānti adhamām gatim
Word by word
Having fallen into a demonic womb, deluded in birth after birth, not reaching me at all, O son of Kuntī, they then go to a still lower state.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘not reaching me’: there is no question of their reaching the Lord; the meaning is, not reaching the right path that he has taught.

त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः । कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत् ॥ २१ ॥
trividhaṃ narakasyedaṃ dvāraṃ nāśanamātmanaḥ | kāmaḥ krodhastathā lobhas tasmādetattrayaṃ tyajet || 21 ||
Padaccheda
त्रिविधम् नरकस्य इदम् द्वारम् नाशनम् आत्मनः कामः क्रोधः तथा लोभः तस्मात् एतत् त्रयम् त्यजेत्
trividham narakasya idam dvāram nāśanam ātmanaḥ kāmaḥ krodhaḥ tathā lobhaḥ tasmāt etat trayam tyajet
Word by word
Threefold is this gate of hell, the ruin of oneself: desire, anger and greed. Therefore one should give up these three.

Note Śaṅkara: this sums up the whole demonic endowment; all its endless kinds are contained in these three, and it is avoided by avoiding them. One is ruined on merely entering this gate: one becomes unfit for any human end.

एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः । आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥ २२ ॥
etairvimuktaḥ kaunteya tamodvāraistribhirnaraḥ | ācaratyātmanaḥ śreyas tato yāti parāṃ gatim || 22 ||
Padaccheda
एतैः विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमः-द्वारैः त्रिभिः नरः आचरति आत्मनः श्रेयः ततः याति पराम् गतिम्
etaiḥ vimuktaḥ kaunteya tamaḥ-dvāraiḥ tribhiḥ naraḥ ācarati ātmanaḥ śreyaḥ tataḥ yāti parām gatim
Word by word
Freed from these three gates of darkness, O son of Kuntī, a man practises his own good; then he goes to the supreme goal.

Note Śaṅkara: darkness is hell, made of pain and delusion. Held back by these he did not practise his good before; with them gone he does, and by that he reaches even liberation.

यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः । न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम् ॥ २३ ॥
yaḥ śāstravidhimutsṛjya vartate kāmakārataḥ | na sa siddhimavāpnoti na sukhaṃ na parāṃ gatim || 23 ||
Padaccheda
यः शास्त्र-विधिम् उत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः न सः सिद्धिम् अवाप्नोति न सुखम् न पराम् गतिम्
yaḥ śāstra-vidhim utsṛjya vartate kāmakārataḥ na saḥ siddhim avāpnoti na sukham na parām gatim
Word by word
He who sets aside the rule of scripture and acts at the prompting of desire attains neither perfection nor happiness nor the supreme goal.

Note Śaṅkara: scripture is the cause of all this avoiding of the demonic endowment and practising of the good. śāstra is the Veda; its rule, injunction and prohibition, is the means of knowing what is and is not to be done. Perfection is fitness for the human end; happiness is in this world; the supreme goal is heaven or liberation.

तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ । ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि ॥ २४ ॥
tasmācchāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ te kāryākāryavyavasthitau | jñātvā śāstravidhānoktaṃ karma kartumihārhasi || 24 ||
Padaccheda
तस्मात् शास्त्रम् प्रमाणम् ते कार्य-अकार्य-व्यवस्थितौ ज्ञात्वा शास्त्र-विधान-उक्तम् कर्म कर्तुम् इह अर्हसि
tasmāt śāstram pramāṇam te kārya-akārya-vyavasthitau jñātvā śāstra-vidhāna-uktam karma kartum iha arhasi
Word by word
Therefore scripture is your authority in settling what is and is not to be done. Having known the action laid down by the rule of scripture, you ought to do it here.

Note Śaṅkara: pramāṇa is the means of knowing. The action is your own action, as scripture lays down ‘one should do’ and ‘one should not do’. ‘Here’ points to the field in which one is entitled to act.
