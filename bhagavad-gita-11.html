एकादशोऽध्यायः
Ekādaśo 'dhyāyaḥ · Adhyāya 11 · Viśvarūpadarśanayoga
अर्जुन उवाचarjuna uvāca
मदनुग्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम् । यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम ॥ १ ॥
madanugrahāya paramaṃ guhyamadhyātmasaṃjñitam | yattvayoktaṃ vacastena moho 'yaṃ vigato mama || 1 ||
Padaccheda
मत्-अनुग्रहाय परमम् गुह्यम् अध्यात्म-संज्ञितम् यत् त्वया उक्तम् वचः तेन मोहः अयम् विगतः मम
mat-anugrahāya paramam guhyam adhyātma-saṃjñitam yat tvayā uktam vacaḥ tena mohaḥ ayam vigataḥ mama
Word by word
Arjuna said: By the word you have spoken out of favour to me, the supreme secret that concerns the Self, this delusion of mine is gone.

Note Śaṅkara: adhyātmasaṃjñitam = whose subject is the discrimination of the Self and the not-Self; ‘the delusion is gone’ = the notion that did not discriminate has left me.

भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया । त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम् ॥ २ ॥
bhavāpyayau hi bhūtānāṃ śrutau vistaraśo mayā | tvattaḥ kamalapatrākṣa māhātmyamapi cāvyayam || 2 ||
Padaccheda
भव-अप्ययौ हि भूतानाम् श्रुतौ विस्तरशः मया त्वत्तः कमल-पत्र-अक्ष माहात्म्यम् अपि च अव्ययम्
bhava-apyayau hi bhūtānām śrutau vistaraśaḥ mayā tvattaḥ kamala-patra-akṣa māhātmyam api ca avyayam
Word by word
For I have heard from you at length of the origin and dissolution of beings, O you with eyes like lotus petals, and also of your imperishable greatness.

Note Śaṅkara: at length, not in brief; with māhātmyam supply ‘has been heard’.

एवमेतद्यथात्थ त्वमात्मानं परमेश्वर । द्रष्टुमिच्छामि ते रूपमैश्वरं पुरुषोत्तम ॥ ३ ॥
evametadyathāttha tvamātmānaṃ parameśvara | draṣṭumicchāmi te rūpamaiśvaraṃ puruṣottama || 3 ||
Padaccheda
एवम् एतत् यथा आत्थ त्वम् आत्मानम् परम-ईश्वर द्रष्टुम् इच्छामि ते रूपम् ऐश्वरम् पुरुष-उत्तम
evam etat yathā āttha tvam ātmānam parama-īśvara draṣṭum icchāmi te rūpam aiśvaram puruṣa-uttama
Word by word
It is so, O supreme Lord, as you declare yourself to be. Yet I wish to see your lordly form, O supreme Puruṣa.

Note Śaṅkara: aiśvaraṃ rūpam = the form of Viṣṇu, endowed with knowledge, lordship, power, strength, valour and splendour.

मन्यसे यदि तच्छक्यं मया द्रष्टुमिति प्रभो । योगेश्वर ततो मे त्वं दर्शयात्मानमव्ययम् ॥ ४ ॥
manyase yadi tacchakyaṃ mayā draṣṭumiti prabho | yogeśvara tato me tvaṃ darśayātmānamavyayam || 4 ||
Padaccheda
मन्यसे यदि तत् शक्यम् मया द्रष्टुम् इति प्रभो योग-ईश्वर ततः मे त्वम् दर्शय आत्मानम् अव्ययम्
manyase yadi tat śakyam mayā draṣṭum iti prabho yoga-īśvara tataḥ me tvam darśaya ātmānam avyayam
Word by word
If you think that it can be seen by me, O Lord, then, O Lord of yogīs, show me your imperishable Self.

Note Śaṅkara: yogeśvara = Lord of the yogīs; ‘then’ = because I long so much to see it.

श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः । नानाविधानि दिव्यानि नानावर्णाकृतीनि च ॥ ५ ॥
paśya me pārtha rūpāṇi śataśo 'tha sahasraśaḥ | nānāvidhāni divyāni nānāvarṇākṛtīni ca || 5 ||
Padaccheda
पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशः अथ सहस्रशः नाना-विधानि दिव्यानि नाना-वर्ण-आकृतीनि च
paśya me pārtha rūpāṇi śataśaḥ atha sahasraśaḥ nānā-vidhāni divyāni nānā-varṇa-ākṛtīni ca
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: Behold, O Pārtha, my forms by hundreds and by thousands, of many kinds, divine, and of many colours and shapes.

Note Śaṅkara: divyāni = arising in heaven, not of the natural order; the colours are blue, yellow and the like, the shapes the particular arrangements of the limbs.

पश्यादित्यान्वसून्रुद्रानश्विनौ मरुतस्तथा । बहून्यदृष्टपूर्वाणि पश्याश्चर्याणि भारत ॥ ६ ॥
paśyādityānvasūnrudrānaśvinau marutastathā | bahūnyadṛṣṭapūrvāṇi paśyāścaryāṇi bhārata || 6 ||
Padaccheda
पश्य आदित्यान् वसून् रुद्रान् अश्विनौ मरुतः तथा बहूनि अदृष्ट-पूर्वाणि पश्य आश्चर्याणि भारत
paśya ādityān vasūn rudrān aśvinau marutaḥ tathā bahūni adṛṣṭa-pūrvāṇi paśya āścaryāṇi bhārata
Word by word
Behold the Ādityas, the Vasus, the Rudras, the two Aśvins and the Maruts; behold, O Bhārata, many wonders never seen before.

Note Śaṅkara: the twelve Ādityas, eight Vasus, eleven Rudras, two Aśvins, and the Maruts in seven groups of seven; never seen before in the world of men, by you or by anyone else.

इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं पश्याद्य सचराचरम् । मम देहे गुडाकेश यच्चान्यद्द्रष्टुमिच्छसि ॥ ७ ॥
ihaikasthaṃ jagatkṛtsnaṃ paśyādya sacarācaram | mama dehe guḍākeśa yaccānyaddraṣṭumicchasi || 7 ||
Padaccheda
इह एक-स्थम् जगत् कृत्स्नम् पश्य अद्य स-चर-अचरम् मम देहे गुडाकेश यत् च अन्यत् द्रष्टुम् इच्छसि
iha eka-stham jagat kṛtsnam paśya adya sa-cara-acaram mama dehe guḍākeśa yat ca anyat draṣṭum icchasi
Word by word
Behold now, O Guḍākeśa, the whole world, moving and unmoving, standing together here in my body, and whatever else you wish to see.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘whatever else’ = victory, defeat and the like, about which Arjuna was in doubt at 2.6 (‘whether we should conquer them or they should conquer us’).

न तु मां शक्यसे द्रष्टुमनेनैव स्वचक्षुषा । दिव्यं ददामि ते चक्षुः पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ॥ ८ ॥
na tu māṃ śakyase draṣṭumanenaiva svacakṣuṣā | divyaṃ dadāmi te cakṣuḥ paśya me yogamaiśvaram || 8 ||
Padaccheda
न तु माम् शक्यसे द्रष्टुम् अनेन एव स्व-चक्षुषा दिव्यम् ददामि ते चक्षुः पश्य मे योगम् ऐश्वरम्
na tu mām śakyase draṣṭum anena eva sva-cakṣuṣā divyam dadāmi te cakṣuḥ paśya me yogam aiśvaram
Word by word
But you cannot see me with this eye of yours alone. I give you a divine eye; behold my divine power.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘me’ = bearing the form of the universe; ‘this eye’ = the natural one; yogam aiśvaram = the surpassing power of yoga that belongs to the Lord.

संजय उवाचsaṃjaya uvāca
एवमुक्त्वा ततो राजन्महायोगेश्वरो हरिः । दर्शयामास पार्थाय परमं रूपमैश्वरम् ॥ ९ ॥
evamuktvā tato rājanmahāyogeśvaro hariḥ | darśayāmāsa pārthāya paramaṃ rūpamaiśvaram || 9 ||
Padaccheda
एवम् उक्त्वा ततः राजन् महा-योग-ईश्वरः हरिः दर्शयामास पार्थाय परमम् रूपम् ऐश्वरम्
evam uktvā tataḥ rājan mahā-yoga-īśvaraḥ hariḥ darśayāmāsa pārthāya paramam rūpam aiśvaram
Word by word
Saṃjaya said: Having spoken thus, O king, Hari, the great Lord of yogīs, then showed to Pārtha his supreme lordly form,

Note Śaṅkara: Hari = Nārāyaṇa; the supreme form = the form of the universe (viśvarūpa).

अनेकवक्त्रनयनमनेकाद्भुतदर्शनम् । अनेकदिव्याभरणं दिव्यानेकोद्यतायुधम् ॥ १० ॥
anekavaktranayanamanekādbhutadarśanam | anekadivyābharaṇaṃ divyānekodyatāyudham || 10 ||
Padaccheda
अनेक-वक्त्र-नयनम् अनेक-अद्भुत-दर्शनम् अनेक-दिव्य-आभरणम् दिव्य-अनेक-उद्यत-आयुधम्
aneka-vaktra-nayanam aneka-adbhuta-darśanam aneka-divya-ābharaṇam divya-aneka-udyata-āyudham
Word by word
with many mouths and eyes, with many wondrous sights, with many divine ornaments, with many divine weapons upraised,

Note Śaṅkara: the weapons are swords and the like; the verse is construed with ‘showed’ of the verse before.

दिव्यमाल्याम्बरधरं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम् । सर्वाश्चर्यमयं देवमनन्तं विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ ११ ॥
divyamālyāmbaradharaṃ divyagandhānulepanam | sarvāścaryamayaṃ devamanantaṃ viśvatomukham || 11 ||
Padaccheda
दिव्य-माल्य-अम्बर-धरम् दिव्य-गन्ध-अनुलेपनम् सर्व-आश्चर्य-मयम् देवम् अनन्तम् विश्वतः-मुखम्
divya-mālya-ambara-dharam divya-gandha-anulepanam sarva-āścarya-mayam devam anantam viśvataḥ-mukham
Word by word
wearing divine garlands and garments, anointed with divine perfumes, full of all wonders, the god, endless, with faces on every side.

Note Śaṅkara: sarvāścaryamayam = made up mostly of wonders; viśvatomukham, because he is the Self of all beings. Supply ‘he showed’, or ‘Arjuna saw’.

दिवि सूर्यसहस्रस्य भवेद्युगपदुत्थिता । यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्याद्भासस्तस्य महात्मनः ॥ १२ ॥
divi sūryasahasrasya bhavedyugapadutthitā | yadi bhāḥ sadṛśī sā syādbhāsastasya mahātmanaḥ || 12 ||
Padaccheda
दिवि सूर्य-सहस्रस्य भवेत् युगपत् उत्थिता यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्यात् भासः तस्य महा-आत्मनः
divi sūrya-sahasrasya bhavet yugapat utthitā yadi bhāḥ sadṛśī sā syāt bhāsaḥ tasya mahā-ātmanaḥ
Word by word
If the light of a thousand suns were to rise at once in the sky, that would be like the light of that great one.

Note Śaṅkara: or it would not be like it; the meaning is that the light of the form of the universe surpasses even that.

तत्रैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं प्रविभक्तमनेकधा । अपश्यद्देवदेवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवस्तदा ॥ १३ ॥
tatraikasthaṃ jagatkṛtsnaṃ pravibhaktamanekadhā | apaśyaddevadevasya śarīre pāṇḍavastadā || 13 ||
Padaccheda
तत्र एक-स्थम् जगत् कृत्स्नम् प्रविभक्तम् अनेकधा अपश्यत् देव-देवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवः तदा
tatra eka-stham jagat kṛtsnam pravibhaktam anekadhā apaśyat deva-devasya śarīre pāṇḍavaḥ tadā
Word by word
There, in the body of the God of gods, the son of Pāṇḍu then saw the whole world standing together, divided in many ways.

Note Śaṅkara: divided as gods, ancestors, men and the rest; the God of gods = Hari.

ततः स विस्मयाविष्टो हृष्टरोमा धनंजयः । प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं कृताञ्जलिरभाषत ॥ १४ ॥
tataḥ sa vismayāviṣṭo hṛṣṭaromā dhanaṃjayaḥ | praṇamya śirasā devaṃ kṛtāñjalirabhāṣata || 14 ||
Padaccheda
ततः सः विस्मय-आविष्टः हृष्ट-रोमा धनंजयः प्रणम्य शिरसा देवम् कृत-अञ्जलिः अभाषत
tataḥ saḥ vismaya-āviṣṭaḥ hṛṣṭa-romā dhanaṃjayaḥ praṇamya śirasā devam kṛta-añjaliḥ abhāṣata
Word by word
Then Dhanaṃjaya, filled with amazement, his hair standing on end, bowed his head to the god and spoke with joined palms.

Note Śaṅkara: the god = the one bearing the form of the universe; Arjuna now makes known his own experience: ‘I see the form of the universe that you have shown’.

अर्जुन उवाचarjuna uvāca
पश्यामि देवांस्तव देव देहे सर्वांस्तथा भूतविशेषसंघान् । ब्रह्माणमीशं कमलासनस्थमृषींश्च सर्वानुरगांश्च दिव्यान् ॥ १५ ॥
paśyāmi devāṃstava deva dehe sarvāṃstathā bhūtaviśeṣasaṃghān | brahmāṇamīśaṃ kamalāsanasthamṛṣīṃśca sarvānuragāṃśca divyān || 15 ||
Padaccheda
पश्यामि देवान् तव देव देहे सर्वान् तथा भूत-विशेष-संघान् ब्रह्माणम् ईशम् कमल-आसन-स्थम् ऋषीन् च सर्वान् उरगान् च दिव्यान्
paśyāmi devān tava deva dehe sarvān tathā bhūta-viśeṣa-saṃghān brahmāṇam īśam kamala-āsana-stham ṛṣīn ca sarvān uragān ca divyān
Word by word
Arjuna said: I see in your body, O God, all the gods and the hosts of the various kinds of beings, Brahmā the lord seated on the lotus seat, all the seers and the divine serpents.

Note Śaṅkara: the beings are the unmoving and the moving, of various shapes; Brahmā the four-faced, the lord of creatures, is seated on Meru, the pericarp of the lotus that is the earth; the seers are Vasiṣṭha and others, the serpents Vāsuki and others.

अनेकबाहूदरवक्त्रनेत्रं पश्यामि त्वा सर्वतोऽनन्तरूपम् । नान्तं न मध्यं न पुनस्तवादिं पश्यामि विश्वेश्वर विश्वरूप ॥ १६ ॥
anekabāhūdaravaktranetraṃ paśyāmi tvā sarvato 'nantarūpam | nāntaṃ na madhyaṃ na punastavādiṃ paśyāmi viśveśvara viśvarūpa || 16 ||
Padaccheda
अनेक-बाहु-उदर-वक्त्र-नेत्रम् पश्यामि त्वा सर्वतः अनन्त-रूपम् न अन्तम् न मध्यम् न पुनः तव आदिम् पश्यामि विश्व-ईश्वर विश्व-रूप
aneka-bāhu-udara-vaktra-netram paśyāmi tvā sarvataḥ ananta-rūpam na antam na madhyam na punaḥ tava ādim paśyāmi viśva-īśvara viśva-rūpa
Word by word
I see you with many arms, bellies, mouths and eyes, of endless forms on every side. I see neither your end nor your middle nor yet your beginning, O Lord of the universe, O you whose form is the universe.
किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रिणं च तेजोराशिं सर्वतो दीप्तिमन्तम् । पश्यामि त्वां दुर्निरीक्ष्यं समन्ताद्दीप्तानलार्कद्युतिमप्रमेयम् ॥ १७ ॥
kirīṭinaṃ gadinaṃ cakriṇaṃ ca tejorāśiṃ sarvato dīptimantam | paśyāmi tvāṃ durnirīkṣyaṃ samantād dīptānalārkadyutim aprameyam || 17 ||
Padaccheda
किरीटिनम् गदिनम् चक्रिणम् च तेजः-राशिम् सर्वतः दीप्तिमन्तम् पश्यामि त्वाम् दुर्निरीक्ष्यम् समन्तात् दीप्त-अनल-अर्क-द्युतिम् अप्रमेयम्
kirīṭinam gadinam cakriṇam ca tejaḥ-rāśim sarvataḥ dīptimantam paśyāmi tvām durnirīkṣyam samantāt dīpta-anala-arka-dyutim aprameyam
Word by word
I see you wearing a diadem, bearing a mace and a discus, a mass of radiance shining on every side, hard to look at, with the brilliance of blazing fire and sun all around, immeasurable.

Note Śaṅkara: he takes sarvato-dīptimantam as one word, ‘having light on every side’; aprameyam = that cannot be delimited.

त्वमक्षरं परमं वेदितव्यं त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् । त्वमव्ययः शाश्वतधर्मगोप्ता सनातनस्त्वं पुरुषो मतो मे ॥ १८ ॥
tvamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ veditavyaṃ tvamasya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam | tvamavyayaḥ śāśvatadharmagoptā sanātanastvaṃ puruṣo mato me || 18 ||
Padaccheda
त्वम् अक्षरम् परमम् वेदितव्यम् त्वम् अस्य विश्वस्य परम् निधानम् त्वम् अव्ययः शाश्वत-धर्म-गोप्ता सनातनः त्वम् पुरुषः मतः मे
tvam akṣaram paramam veditavyam tvam asya viśvasya param nidhānam tvam avyayaḥ śāśvata-dharma-goptā sanātanaḥ tvam puruṣaḥ mataḥ me
Word by word
You are the imperishable, the supreme Brahman that is to be known; you are the supreme resting place of this universe; you are the undecaying guardian of the eternal dharma; you are the everlasting Puruṣa, so I hold.

Note Śaṅkara: paramam = the supreme Brahman, to be known by those who seek liberation; nidhānam = that in which a thing is laid down, the supreme support.

अनादिमध्यान्तमनन्तवीर्यमनन्तबाहुं शशिसूर्यनेत्रम् । पश्यामि त्वां दीप्तहुताशवक्त्रं स्वतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम् ॥ १९ ॥
anādimadhyāntamanantavīryamanantabāhuṃ śaśisūryanetram | paśyāmi tvāṃ dīptahutāśavaktraṃ svatejasā viśvamidaṃ tapantam || 19 ||

Note Śaṅkara: the fame is that Arjuna conquered Bhīṣma and the other great warriors, whom even the gods could not conquer; samṛddham = without a rival; Arjuna is called Savyasācin because he shoots arrows with the left hand as well.

द्रोणं च भीष्मं च जयद्रथं च कर्णं तथान्यानपि योधवीरान् । मया हतांस्त्वं जहि मा व्यथिष्ठा युध्यस्व जेतासि रणे सपत्नान् ॥ ३४ ॥
droṇaṃ ca bhīṣmaṃ ca jayadrathaṃ ca karṇaṃ tathānyānapi yodhavīrān | mayā hatāṃstvaṃ jahi mā vyathiṣṭhā yudhyasva jetāsi raṇe sapatnān || 34 ||
Padaccheda
द्रोणम् च भीष्मम् च जयद्रथम् च कर्णम् तथा अन्यान् अपि योध-वीरान् मया हतान् त्वम् जहि मा व्यथिष्ठाः युध्यस्व जेतासि रणे सपत्नान्
droṇam ca bhīṣmam ca jayadratham ca karṇam tathā anyān api yodha-vīrān mayā hatān tvam jahi mā vyathiṣṭhāḥ yudhyasva jetāsi raṇe sapatnān
Word by word
Slay Droṇa, Bhīṣma, Jayadratha, Karṇa and the other heroic warriors too, who are already slain by me. Do not be afraid. Fight; you will conquer your rivals in battle.

Note Śaṅkara: the Lord names those about whom Arjuna had misgivings. Droṇa is the teacher of archery, armed with divine weapons, and Arjuna's own teacher; Bhīṣma dies only when he chooses and was not defeated by Paraśurāma in single combat; Jayadratha's father performs austerity so that the head of whoever makes his son's head fall to the ground shall fall too; Karṇa, son of the Sun, holds the unfailing spear given by Indra.

संजय उवाचsaṃjaya uvāca
एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं केशवस्य कृताञ्जलिर्वेपमानः किरीटी । नमस्कृत्वा भूय एवाह कृष्णं सगद्गदं भीतभीतः प्रणम्य ॥ ३५ ॥
etacchrutvā vacanaṃ keśavasya kṛtāñjalirvepamānaḥ kirīṭī | namaskṛtvā bhūya evāha kṛṣṇaṃ sagadgadaṃ bhītabhītaḥ praṇamya || 35 ||
Padaccheda
एतत् श्रुत्वा वचनम् केशवस्य कृत-अञ्जलिः वेपमानः किरीटी नमस्कृत्वा भूयः एव आह कृष्णम् सगद्गदम् भीत-भीतः प्रणम्य
etat śrutvā vacanam keśavasya kṛta-añjaliḥ vepamānaḥ kirīṭī namaskṛtvā bhūyaḥ eva āha kṛṣṇam sagadgadam bhīta-bhītaḥ praṇamya
Word by word
Saṃjaya said: Having heard this speech of Keśava, the diademed one, trembling, with joined palms, made obeisance and, bowing down, greatly afraid, spoke again to Kṛṣṇa in a faltering voice.

Note Śaṅkara: the voice falters because the throat is choked as the eyes fill with tears: from the blow of pain in one seized by fear, from the rise of joy in one seized by affection. sagadgadam qualifies the act of speaking; bhītabhītaḥ = his mind seized by fear again and again.

अर्जुन उवाचarjuna uvāca
स्थाने हृषीकेश तव प्रकीर्त्या जगत्प्रहृष्यत्यनुरज्यते च । रक्षांसि भीतानि दिशो द्रवन्ति सर्वे नमस्यन्ति च सिद्धसंघाः ॥ ३६ ॥
sthāne hṛṣīkeśa tava prakīrtyā jagatprahṛṣyatyanurajyate ca | rakṣāṃsi bhītāni diśo dravanti sarve namasyanti ca siddhasaṃghāḥ || 36 ||
Padaccheda
स्थाने हृषीकेश तव प्रकीर्त्या जगत् प्रहृष्यति अनुरज्यते च रक्षांसि भीतानि दिशः द्रवन्ति सर्वे नमस्यन्ति च सिद्ध-संघाः
sthāne hṛṣīkeśa tava prakīrtyā jagat prahṛṣyati anurajyate ca rakṣāṃsi bhītāni diśaḥ dravanti sarve namasyanti ca siddha-saṃghāḥ
Word by word
Arjuna said: It is right, O Hṛṣīkeśa, that the world rejoices and is filled with love at the praise of you, that the Rākṣasas flee in fear to the quarters, and that all the hosts of perfected ones bow down.

Note Śaṅkara: prakīrtyā = by hearing your greatness told. Or sthāne qualifies the object: the Lord is the fit object of joy and the rest, since he is the Lord, the Self of all and the friend of all beings. The perfected ones are Kapila and others.

कस्माच्च ते न नमेरन्महात्मन्गरीयसे ब्रह्मणोऽप्यादिकर्त्रे । अनन्त देवेश जगन्निवास त्वमक्षरं सदसत्तत्परं यत् ॥ ३७ ॥
kasmācca te na nameranmahātmangarīyase brahmaṇo 'pyādikartre | ananta deveśa jagannivāsa tvamakṣaraṃ sadasattatparaṃ yat || 37 ||
Padaccheda
कस्मात् च ते न नमेरन् महा-आत्मन् गरीयसे ब्रह्मणः अपि आदि-कर्त्रे अनन्त देव-ईश जगत्-निवास त्वम् अक्षरम् सत् असत् तत्-परम् यत्
kasmāt ca te na nameran mahā-ātman garīyase brahmaṇaḥ api ādi-kartre ananta deva-īśa jagat-nivāsa tvam akṣaram sat asat tat-param yat
Word by word
And why should they not bow to you, O great one, who are greater, the first cause even of Brahmā? O endless one, O Lord of gods, O abode of the world, you are the imperishable: the existent and the non-existent, and that which is beyond them.

Note Śaṅkara: Brahmā = Hiraṇyagarbha. sat = what exists, asat = that of which there is the notion ‘it is not’; these two are limiting adjuncts of the imperishable, through which it is spoken of as existent and non-existent. In truth the imperishable, of which the knowers of the Veda speak (8.11), is beyond both; that is you alone.

त्वमादिदेवः पुरुषः पुराणस्त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् । वेत्तासि वेद्यं च परं च धाम त्वया ततं विश्वमनन्तरूप ॥ ३८ ॥
tvamādidevaḥ puruṣaḥ purāṇastvam asya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam | vettāsi vedyaṃ ca paraṃ ca dhāma tvayā tataṃ viśvamanantarūpa || 38 ||
Padaccheda
त्वम् आदि-देवः पुरुषः पुराणः त्वम् अस्य विश्वस्य परम् निधानम् वेत्ता असि वेद्यम् च परम् च धाम त्वया ततम् विश्वम् अनन्त-रूप
tvam ādi-devaḥ puruṣaḥ purāṇaḥ tvam asya viśvasya param nidhānam vettā asi vedyam ca param ca dhāma tvayā tatam viśvam ananta-rūpa
Word by word
You are the first God, the ancient Puruṣa; you are the supreme resting place of this universe. You are the knower and what is to be known, and the supreme abode. By you the universe is pervaded, O you of endless forms.

Note Śaṅkara: ādideva, because he is the creator of the world; puruṣa, because he lies in the city (the body); nidhānam = that in which the whole world is laid down at the great dissolution; paraṃ dhāma = the supreme state of Viṣṇu.

वायुर्यमोऽग्निर्वरुणः शशाङ्कः प्रजापतिस्त्वं प्रपितामहश्च । नमो नमस्तेऽस्तु सहस्रकृत्वः पुनश्च भूयोऽपि नमो नमस्ते ॥ ३९ ॥
vāyuryamo 'gnirvaruṇaḥ śaśāṅkaḥ prajāpatistvaṃ prapitāmahaśca | namo namaste 'stu sahasrakṛtvaḥ punaśca bhūyo 'pi namo namaste || 39 ||
Padaccheda
वायुः यमः अग्निः वरुणः शश-अङ्कः प्रजापतिः त्वम् प्रपितामहः च नमः नमः ते अस्तु सहस्र-कृत्वः पुनः च भूयः अपि नमः नमः ते
vāyuḥ yamaḥ agniḥ varuṇaḥ śaśa-aṅkaḥ prajāpatiḥ tvam prapitāmahaḥ ca namaḥ namaḥ te astu sahasra-kṛtvaḥ punaḥ ca bhūyaḥ api namaḥ namaḥ te
Word by word
You are Vāyu, Yama, Agni, Varuṇa, the Moon, Prajāpati and the great-grandfather. Homage, homage to you a thousand times, and again and yet again homage, homage to you.

Note Śaṅkara: Prajāpati = Kaśyapa and the others; prapitāmaha = the father even of the grandfather, that is, of Brahmā; ‘again and yet again’ shows that, from the excess of his faith and devotion, he is not satisfied.

नमः पुरस्तादथ पृष्ठतस्ते नमोऽस्तु ते सर्वत एव सर्व । अनन्तवीर्यामितविक्रमस्त्वं सर्वं समाप्नोषि ततोऽसि सर्वः ॥ ४० ॥
namaḥ purastādatha pṛṣṭhataste namo 'stu te sarvata eva sarva | anantavīryāmitavikramastvaṃ sarvaṃ samāpnoṣi tato 'si sarvaḥ || 40 ||
Padaccheda
नमः पुरस्तात् अथ पृष्ठतः ते नमः अस्तु ते सर्वतः एव सर्व अनन्त-वीर्य-अमित-विक्रमः त्वम् सर्वम् समाप्नोषि ततः असि सर्वः
namaḥ purastāt atha pṛṣṭhataḥ te namaḥ astu te sarvataḥ eva sarva ananta-vīrya-amita-vikramaḥ tvam sarvam samāpnoṣi tataḥ asi sarvaḥ
Word by word
Homage to you in front and behind; homage to you on every side, O All. Of endless might and measureless valour, you pervade all, and therefore you are all.

Note Śaṅkara: vīrya = ability, vikrama = prowess: one may have might and yet show no prowess, or little, in slaying enemies and the like. ‘You are all’ = nothing exists apart from you.

सखेति मत्वा प्रसभं यदुक्तं हे कृष्ण हे यादव हे सखेति । अजानता महिमानं तवेदं मया प्रमादात्प्रणयेन वापि ॥ ४१ ॥
sakheti matvā prasabhaṃ yaduktaṃ he kṛṣṇa he yādava he sakheti | ajānatā mahimānaṃ tavedaṃ mayā pramādātpraṇayena vāpi || 41 ||
Padaccheda
सखा इति मत्वा प्रसभम् यत् उक्तम् हे कृष्ण हे यादव हे सखे इति अजानता महिमानम् तव इदम् मया प्रमादात् प्रणयेन वा अपि
sakhā iti matvā prasabham yat uktam he kṛṣṇa he yādava he sakhe iti ajānatā mahimānam tava idam mayā pramādāt praṇayena vā api
Word by word
Whatever I said rashly, thinking you a friend, ‘O Kṛṣṇa, O Yādava, O friend’, not knowing this greatness of yours, from carelessness or even out of affection,

Note Śaṅkara: sakhā = one of the same age; idam = this form of the universe, the Lord's. ‘This’ (neuter) and ‘greatness’ (masculine) are construed though they do not agree; he mentions the reading tavemam, with which they agree. pramāda = a distracted mind; praṇaya = the familiarity that comes from affection.

Note The second sakheti is sakhe + iti (vocative); DCS analyses it as sakhā iti.

यच्चावहासार्थमसत्कृतोऽसि विहारशय्यासनभोजनेषु । एकोऽथ वाप्यच्युत तत्समक्षं तत्क्षामये त्वामहमप्रमेयम् ॥ ४२ ॥
yaccāvahāsārthamasatkṛto 'si vihāraśayyāsanabhojaneṣu | eko 'tha vāpyacyuta tatsamakṣaṃ tatkṣāmaye tvām aham aprameyam || 42 ||
Padaccheda
यत् च अवहास-अर्थम् असत्कृतः असि विहार-शय्या-आसन-भोजनेषु एकः अथ वा अपि अच्युत तत् समक्षम् तत् क्षामये त्वाम् अहम् अप्रमेयम्
yat ca avahāsa-artham asatkṛtaḥ asi vihāra-śayyā-āsana-bhojaneṣu ekaḥ atha vā api acyuta tat samakṣam tat kṣāmaye tvām aham aprameyam
Word by word
and in that you were treated with disrespect in jest, while walking, lying, sitting or eating, when alone, O Acyuta, or before the eyes of others, for all that I ask forgiveness of you, the immeasurable.

Note Śaṅkara: ekaḥ = out of the sight of others, samakṣam = before their eyes, the word tat being adverbial; tat (second) = all that mass of offences; aprameyam = beyond the means of knowledge.

पितासि लोकस्य चराचरस्य त्वमस्य पूज्यश्च गुरुर्गरीयान् । न त्वत्समोऽस्त्यभ्यधिकः कुतोऽन्यो लोकत्रयेऽप्यप्रतिमप्रभाव ॥ ४३ ॥
pitāsi lokasya carācarasya tvamasya pūjyaśca gururgarīyān | na tvatsamo 'styabhyadhikaḥ kuto 'nyo lokatraye 'pyapratimaprabhāva || 43 ||
Padaccheda
पिता असि लोकस्य चर-अचरस्य त्वम् अस्य पूज्यः च गुरुः गरीयान् न त्वत्-समः अस्ति अभ्यधिकः कुतः अन्यः लोक-त्रये अपि अप्रतिम-प्रभाव
pitā asi lokasya cara-acarasya tvam asya pūjyaḥ ca guruḥ garīyān na tvat-samaḥ asti abhyadhikaḥ kutaḥ anyaḥ loka-traye api apratima-prabhāva
Word by word
You are the father of this world, moving and unmoving; you are worthy of its worship, for you are the greater Guru. There is none equal to you; how then could there be another greater, even in the three worlds, O you of matchless power?

Note Śaṅkara: worthy of worship because he is the greater Guru. None is equal, for two Lords are not possible: with more than one Lord the dealings of the world could not go on.

तस्मात्प्रणम्य प्रणिधाय कायं प्रसादये त्वामहमीशमीड्यम् । पितेव पुत्रस्य सखेव सख्युः प्रियः प्रियायार्हसि देव सोढुम् ॥ ४४ ॥
tasmātpraṇamya praṇidhāya kāyaṃ prasādaye tvāmahamīśamīḍyam | piteva putrasya sakheva sakhyuḥ priyaḥ priyāyārhasi deva soḍhum || 44 ||
Padaccheda
तस्मात् प्रणम्य प्रणिधाय कायम् प्रसादये त्वाम् अहम् ईशम् ईड्यम् पिता इव पुत्रस्य सखा इव सख्युः प्रियः प्रियायाः अर्हसि देव सोढुम्
tasmāt praṇamya praṇidhāya kāyam prasādaye tvām aham īśam īḍyam pitā iva putrasya sakhā iva sakhyuḥ priyaḥ priyāyāḥ arhasi deva soḍhum
Word by word
Therefore, bowing and laying my body low, I seek your grace, the Lord worthy of praise. As a father bears with a son, a friend with a friend, a lover with his beloved, so should you bear with me, O God.

Note Śaṅkara: with each genitive supply ‘the offence’; soḍhum = to forgive.

Note priyāyārhasi is taken, with Śaṅkara, as priyāyāḥ arhasi with irregular sandhi; DCS reads the dative priyāya.

अदृष्टपूर्वं हृषितोऽस्मि दृष्ट्वा भयेन च प्रव्यथितं मनो मे । तदेव मे दर्शय देव रूपं प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास ॥ ४५ ॥
adṛṣṭapūrvaṃ hṛṣito 'smi dṛṣṭvā bhayena ca pravyathitaṃ mano me | tadeva me darśaya deva rūpaṃ prasīda deveśa jagannivāsa || 45 ||
Padaccheda
अदृष्ट-पूर्वम् हृषितः अस्मि दृष्ट्वा भयेन च प्रव्यथितम् मनः मे तत् एव मे दर्शय देव रूपम् प्रसीद देव-ईश जगत्-निवास
adṛṣṭa-pūrvam hṛṣitaḥ asmi dṛṣṭvā bhayena ca pravyathitam manaḥ me tat eva me darśaya deva rūpam prasīda deva-īśa jagat-nivāsa
Word by word
I am delighted at having seen what was never seen before, and yet my mind is shaken with fear. Show me that very form, O God; be gracious, O Lord of gods, O abode of the world.

Note Śaṅkara: never seen before, by me or by others; ‘that very form’ = the form of my friend.

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रहस्तमिच्छामि त्वां द्रष्टुमहं तथैव । तेनैव रूपेण चतुर्भुजेन सहस्रबाहो भव विश्वमूर्ते ॥ ४६ ॥
kirīṭinaṃ gadinaṃ cakrahastamicchāmi tvāṃ draṣṭumahaṃ tathaiva | tenaiva rūpeṇa caturbhujena sahasrabāho bhava viśvamūrte || 46 ||
Padaccheda
किरीटिनम् गदिनम् चक्र-हस्तम् इच्छामि त्वाम् द्रष्टुम् अहम् तथा एव तेन एव रूपेण चतुर्-भुजेन सहस्र-बाहो भव विश्व-मूर्ते
kirīṭinam gadinam cakra-hastam icchāmi tvām draṣṭum aham tathā eva tena eva rūpeṇa catur-bhujena sahasra-bāho bhava viśva-mūrte
Word by word
I wish to see you just as before, wearing the diadem, bearing the mace, discus in hand. Take that very four-armed form, O thousand-armed, O you whose body is the universe.

Note Śaṅkara: tathaiva = as before; ‘that very form’ = the form of Vasudeva's son; the meaning is: withdraw the form of the universe and be of that form.

श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
मया प्रसन्नेन तवार्जुनेदं रूपं परं दर्शितमात्मयोगात् । तेजोमयं विश्वमनन्तमाद्यं यन्मे त्वदन्येन न दृष्टपूर्वम् ॥ ४७ ॥
mayā prasannena tavārjunedaṃ rūpaṃ paraṃ darśitamātmayogāt | tejomayaṃ viśvamanantamādyaṃ yanme tvadanyena na dṛṣṭapūrvam || 47 ||
Padaccheda
मया प्रसन्नेन तव अर्जुन इदम् रूपम् परम् दर्शितम् आत्म-योगात् तेजः-मयम् विश्वम् अनन्तम् आद्यम् यत् मे त्वत्-अन्येन न दृष्ट-पूर्वम्
mayā prasannena tava arjuna idam rūpam param darśitam ātma-yogāt tejaḥ-mayam viśvam anantam ādyam yat me tvat-anyena na dṛṣṭa-pūrvam
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: Being gracious to you, O Arjuna, I have shown you by my own power this supreme form, full of radiance, entire, endless, primal, which no one but you has seen before.

Note Śaṅkara: prasāda = the will to show favour; ātmayogāt = by the power of his own lordship; viśvam = whole.

न वेदयज्ञाध्ययनैर्न दानैर्न च क्रियाभिर्न तपोभिरुग्रैः । एवंरूपः शक्य अहं नृलोके द्रष्टुं त्वदन्येन कुरुप्रवीर ॥ ४८ ॥
na vedayajñādhyayanairna dānairna ca kriyābhirna tapobhirugraiḥ | evaṃrūpaḥ śakya ahaṃ nṛloke draṣṭuṃ tvadanyena kurupravīra || 48 ||
Padaccheda
न वेद-यज्ञ-अध्ययनैः न दानैः न च क्रियाभिः न तपोभिः उग्रैः एवं-रूपः शक्यः अहम् नृ-लोके द्रष्टुम् त्वत्-अन्येन कुरु-प्रवीर
na veda-yajña-adhyayanaiḥ na dānaiḥ na ca kriyābhiḥ na tapobhiḥ ugraiḥ evaṃ-rūpaḥ śakyaḥ aham nṛ-loke draṣṭum tvat-anyena kuru-pravīra
Word by word
Not by study of the Vedas and of sacrifices, not by gifts, not by rites, not by severe austerities can I be seen in such a form in the world of men by anyone but you, O hero of the Kurus.

Note Śaṅkara: study of sacrifices is named apart, though it is included in the study of the Vedas, to point to the understanding of sacrifice; the gifts are the tulāpuruṣa and the like, the rites the Agnihotra and the like, the austerities the Cāndrāyaṇa and the like.

Note śakya aham stands for śakyo 'ham (so also in 11.54).

मा ते व्यथा मा च विमूढभावो दृष्ट्वा रूपं घोरमीदृङ्ममेदम् । व्यपेतभीः प्रीतमनाः पुनस्त्वं तदेव मे रूपमिदं प्रपश्य ॥ ४९ ॥
mā te vyathā mā ca vimūḍhabhāvo dṛṣṭvā rūpaṃ ghoramīdṛṅmamedam | vyapetabhīḥ prītamanāḥ punastvaṃ tadeva me rūpamidaṃ prapaśya || 49 ||
Padaccheda
मा ते व्यथा मा च विमूढ-भावः दृष्ट्वा रूपम् घोरम् ईदृक् मम इदम् व्यपेत-भीः प्रीत-मनाः पुनः त्वम् तत् एव मे रूपम् इदम् प्रपश्य
mā te vyathā mā ca vimūḍha-bhāvaḥ dṛṣṭvā rūpam ghoram īdṛk mama idam vyapeta-bhīḥ prīta-manāḥ punaḥ tvam tat eva me rūpam idam prapaśya
Word by word
Do not be afraid, do not be bewildered, at seeing this form of mine, so dreadful. Free from fear and glad at heart, behold again that very form of mine.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘that very form’ = the four-armed form bearing conch, discus and mace, which you desire.

संजय उवाचsaṃjaya uvāca
इत्यर्जुनं वासुदेवस्तथोक्त्वा स्वकं रूपं दर्शयामास भूयः । आश्वासयामास च भीतमेनं भूत्वा पुनः सौम्यवपुर्महात्मा ॥ ५० ॥
ityarjunaṃ vāsudevastathoktvā svakaṃ rūpaṃ darśayāmāsa bhūyaḥ | āśvāsayāmāsa ca bhītamenaṃ bhūtvā punaḥ saumyavapurmahātmā || 50 ||
Padaccheda
इति अर्जुनम् वासुदेवः तथा उक्त्वा स्वकम् रूपम् दर्शयामास भूयः आश्वासयामास च भीतम् एनम् भूत्वा पुनः सौम्य-वपुः महा-आत्मा
iti arjunam vāsudevaḥ tathā uktvā svakam rūpam darśayāmāsa bhūyaḥ āśvāsayāmāsa ca bhītam enam bhūtvā punaḥ saumya-vapuḥ mahā-ātmā
Word by word
Saṃjaya said: Having spoken thus to Arjuna, Vāsudeva showed his own form again; and the great one, becoming gentle in body once more, comforted him who was afraid.

Note Śaṅkara: his own form = the one born in Vasudeva's house.

अर्जुन उवाचarjuna uvāca
दृष्ट्वेदं मानुषं रूपं तव सौम्यं जनार्दन । इदानीमस्मि संवृत्तः सचेताः प्रकृतिं गतः ॥ ५१ ॥
dṛṣṭvedaṃ mānuṣaṃ rūpaṃ tava saumyaṃ janārdana | idānīmasmi saṃvṛttaḥ sacetāḥ prakṛtiṃ gataḥ || 51 ||
Padaccheda
दृष्ट्वा इदम् मानुषम् रूपम् तव सौम्यम् जनार्दन इदानीम् अस्मि संवृत्तः सचेताः प्रकृतिम् गतः
dṛṣṭvā idam mānuṣam rūpam tava saumyam janārdana idānīm asmi saṃvṛttaḥ sacetāḥ prakṛtim gataḥ
Word by word
Arjuna said: Seeing this gentle human form of yours, O Janārdana, I have now become composed in mind and have returned to my natural state.

Note Śaṅkara: the human form = that of my friend; sacetāḥ = of settled mind; prakṛti = one's own nature.

श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
सुदुर्दर्शमिदं रूपं दृष्टवानसि यन्मम । देवा अप्यस्य रूपस्य नित्यं दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ ५२ ॥
sudurdarśamidaṃ rūpaṃ dṛṣṭavānasi yanmama | devā apyasya rūpasya nityaṃ darśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ || 52 ||
Padaccheda
सु-दुर्दर्शम् इदम् रूपम् दृष्टवान् असि यत् मम देवाः अपि अस्य रूपस्य नित्यम् दर्शन-काङ्क्षिणः
su-durdarśam idam rūpam dṛṣṭavān asi yat mama devāḥ api asya rūpasya nityam darśana-kāṅkṣiṇaḥ
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: This form of mine that you have seen is very hard to see. Even the gods are ever longing for a sight of this form.

Note Śaṅkara: though they long to see it, they have not seen it as you have, nor will they.

नाहं वेदैर्न तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया । शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा ॥ ५३ ॥
nāhaṃ vedairna tapasā na dānena na cejyayā | śakya evaṃvidho draṣṭuṃ dṛṣṭavānasi māṃ yathā || 53 ||
Padaccheda
न अहम् वेदैः न तपसा न दानेन न च इज्यया शक्यः एवं-विधः द्रष्टुम् दृष्टवान् असि माम् यथा
na aham vedaiḥ na tapasā na dānena na ca ijyayā śakyaḥ evaṃ-vidhaḥ draṣṭum dṛṣṭavān asi mām yathā
Word by word
Not by the Vedas, not by austerity, not by giving, nor by sacrifice can I be seen in this way, as you have seen me.

Note Śaṅkara: the four Vedas; giving of cows, land, gold and the like; ijyā = sacrifice, or worship.

भक्त्या त्वनन्यया शक्य अहमेवंविधोऽर्जुन । ज्ञातुं द्रष्टुं च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुं च परंतप ॥ ५४ ॥
bhaktyā tvananyayā śakya ahamevaṃvidho 'rjuna | jñātuṃ draṣṭuṃ ca tattvena praveṣṭuṃ ca paraṃtapa || 54 ||
Padaccheda
भक्त्या तु अनन्यया शक्यः अहम् एवं-विधः अर्जुन ज्ञातुम् द्रष्टुम् च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुम् च परं-तप
bhaktyā tu ananyayā śakyaḥ aham evaṃ-vidhaḥ arjuna jñātum draṣṭum ca tattvena praveṣṭum ca paraṃ-tapa
Word by word
But by undivided devotion, O Arjuna, I can in this way be known, seen in truth and entered into, O scorcher of foes.

Note Śaṅkara: ananyā bhakti = devotion that never turns to anything apart from the Lord, by which nothing other than Vāsudeva is perceived by any of the senses; ‘known’ = from the scriptures; ‘seen in truth’ = directly realized; ‘entered’ = liberation attained.

मत्कर्मकृन्मत्परमो मद्भक्तः सङ्गवर्जितः । निर्वैरः सर्वभूतेषु यः स मामेति पाण्डव ॥ ५५ ॥
matkarmakṛnmatparamo madbhaktaḥ saṅgavarjitaḥ | nirvairaḥ sarvabhūteṣu yaḥ sa māmeti pāṇḍava || 55 ||
Padaccheda
मत्-कर्म-कृत् मत्-परमः मत्-भक्तः सङ्ग-वर्जितः निर्वैरः सर्व-भूतेषु यः सः माम् एति पाण्डव
mat-karma-kṛt mat-paramaḥ mat-bhaktaḥ saṅga-varjitaḥ nirvairaḥ sarva-bhūteṣu yaḥ saḥ mām eti pāṇḍava
Word by word
He who does work for me, who holds me as the supreme goal, who is devoted to me, free from attachment and without enmity towards any being, he comes to me, O son of Pāṇḍu.

Note Śaṅkara: here the essence of the whole teaching of the Gītā, which leads to the highest good, is gathered together as what is to be practised. A servant does his master's work but does not take the master as the goal he is to reach after death; this man does the Lord's work and takes him alone as the supreme goal. Attachment = fondness for wealth, sons, friends, wife and kin; without enmity even towards those bent on doing him the greatest harm.
