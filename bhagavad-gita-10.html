दशमोऽध्यायः
Daśamo 'dhyāyaḥ · Adhyāya 10 · Vibhūtiyoga
श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
भूय एव महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः । यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया ॥ १ ॥
bhūya eva mahābāho śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ | yatte 'haṃ prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hitakāmyayā || 1 ||
Padaccheda
भूयः एव महा-बाहो शृणु मे परमम् वचः यत् ते अहम् प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हित-काम्यया
bhūyaḥ eva mahā-bāho śṛṇu me paramam vacaḥ yat te aham prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hita-kāmyayā
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: Hear again, O mighty-armed, my supreme word, which I shall speak to you, who take delight in it, out of a wish for your good.

Note Śaṅkara: the truth of the Lord and his glories were shown in adhyāyas 7 and 9; now the things in which he is to be thought of are to be told, and his truth again, since it is hard to know. The word is supreme because it reveals the unsurpassed reality. Arjuna delights in it as one drinking nectar.

न मे विदुः सुरगणाः प्रभवं न महर्षयः । अहमादिर्हि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः ॥ २ ॥
na me viduḥ suragaṇāḥ prabhavaṃ na maharṣayaḥ | ahamādirhi devānāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ || 2 ||
Padaccheda
न मे विदुः सुर-गणाः प्रभवम् न महा-ऋषयः अहम् आदिः हि देवानाम् महा-ऋषीणाम् च सर्वशः
na me viduḥ sura-gaṇāḥ prabhavam na mahā-ṛṣayaḥ aham ādiḥ hi devānām mahā-ṛṣīṇām ca sarvaśaḥ
Word by word
Neither the hosts of gods nor the great seers know my origin; for I am in every way the source of the gods and of the great seers.

Note Śaṅkara: the gods are Brahmā and the rest, the seers Bhṛgu and the rest. prabhava is either the Lord's surpassing power of lordship, or his coming into being.

यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम् । असंमूढः स मर्त्येषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३ ॥
yo māmajamanādiṃ ca vetti lokamaheśvaram | asaṃmūḍhaḥ sa martyeṣu sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate || 3 ||
Padaccheda
यः माम् अजम् अनादिम् च वेत्ति लोक-महा-ईश्वरम् असंमूढः सः मर्त्येषु सर्व-पापैः प्रमुच्यते
yaḥ mām ajam anādim ca vetti loka-mahā-īśvaram asaṃmūḍhaḥ saḥ martyeṣu sarva-pāpaiḥ pramucyate
Word by word
He who knows me as unborn and without beginning, the great Lord of the worlds, is undeluded among mortals and is freed from all sins.

Note Śaṅkara: being the source of gods and seers, the Lord has no source of his own; having no beginning is the reason he is unborn. He is the Fourth, free of ignorance and its effects. The sins are those done knowingly and unknowingly.

बुद्धिर्ज्ञानमसंमोहः क्षमा सत्यं दमः शमः । सुखं दुःखं भवोऽभावो भयं चाभयमेव च ॥ ४ ॥
buddhirjñānamasaṃmohaḥ kṣamā satyaṃ damaḥ śamaḥ | sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ bhavo 'bhāvo bhayaṃ cābhayameva ca || 4 ||
Padaccheda
बुद्धिः ज्ञानम् असंमोहः क्षमा सत्यम् दमः शमः सुखम् दुःखम् भवः अभावः भयम् च अभयम् एव च
buddhiḥ jñānam asaṃmohaḥ kṣamā satyam damaḥ śamaḥ sukham duḥkham bhavaḥ abhāvaḥ bhayam ca abhayam eva ca
Word by word
Understanding, knowledge, freedom from delusion, forbearance, truthfulness, self-control, calm, pleasure, pain, arising, ceasing, fear and fearlessness,

Note Śaṅkara: buddhi is the mind's power to grasp subtle things; jñāna the knowledge of the Self and other things; asaṃmoha acting with discernment towards whatever presents itself; kṣamā an unruffled mind when abused or struck; satya speech that conveys to another exactly what one has seen and heard; dama the quieting of the outer senses, śama that of the mind.

अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः । भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः ॥ ५ ॥
ahiṃsā samatā tuṣṭistapo dānaṃ yaśo 'yaśaḥ | bhavanti bhāvā bhūtānāṃ matta eva pṛthagvidhāḥ || 5 ||
Padaccheda
अहिंसा समता तुष्टिः तपः दानम् यशः अ-यशः भवन्ति भावाः भूतानाम् मत्तः एव पृथग्विधाः
ahiṃsā samatā tuṣṭiḥ tapaḥ dānam yaśaḥ a-yaśaḥ bhavanti bhāvāḥ bhūtānām mattaḥ eva pṛthagvidhāḥ
Word by word
non-injury, evenness, contentment, austerity, giving, fame and ill fame: these states of beings, of many kinds, arise from me alone.

Note Śaṅkara: contentment is the sense of having enough in what one gets; austerity is the mortification of the body with restraint of the senses; giving is sharing according to one's means; fame comes from dharma and ill fame from adharma. They arise from the Lord in accordance with each being's own actions.

महर्षयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारो मनवस्तथा । मद्भावा मानसा जाता येषां लोक इमाः प्रजाः ॥ ६ ॥
maharṣayaḥ sapta pūrve catvāro manavastathā | madbhāvā mānasā jātā yeṣāṃ loka imāḥ prajāḥ || 6 ||
Padaccheda
महा-ऋषयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारः मनवः तथा मद्-भावाः मानसाः जाताः येषाम् लोके इमाः प्रजाः
mahā-ṛṣayaḥ sapta pūrve catvāraḥ manavaḥ tathā mad-bhāvāḥ mānasāḥ jātāḥ yeṣām loke imāḥ prajāḥ
Word by word
The seven great seers of old, and also the four Manus, with their minds on me, were born from my mind; of them are these creatures in the world.

Note Śaṅkara: the seers are Bhṛgu and the rest; the four Manus are those known as the Sāvarṇas. madbhāvāḥ: with their thought fixed on the Lord, and so endowed with the power of Viṣṇu. The creatures, moving and unmoving, are the creation of these Manus and seers.

एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः । सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ७ ॥
etāṃ vibhūtiṃ yogaṃ ca mama yo vetti tattvataḥ | so 'vikampena yogena yujyate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 7 ||
Padaccheda
एताम् विभूतिम् योगम् च मम यः वेत्ति तत्त्वतः सः अविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते न अत्र संशयः
etām vibhūtim yogam ca mama yaḥ vetti tattvataḥ saḥ avikampena yogena yujyate na atra saṃśayaḥ
Word by word
He who truly knows this glory and power of mine is endowed with unwavering yoga; there is no doubt about this.

Note Śaṅkara: vibhūti is the Lord's wide extent. The first yoga is his way of working, or the lordly power and all-knowledge that come of yoga; the second, the unwavering yoga, is steadiness in right vision.

अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वं प्रवर्तते । इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः ॥ ८ ॥
ahaṃ sarvasya prabhavo mattaḥ sarvaṃ pravartate | iti matvā bhajante māṃ budhā bhāvasamanvitāḥ || 8 ||
Padaccheda
अहम् सर्वस्य प्रभवः मत्तः सर्वम् प्रवर्तते इति मत्वा भजन्ते माम् बुधाः भाव-समन्विताः
aham sarvasya prabhavaḥ mattaḥ sarvam pravartate iti matvā bhajante mām budhāḥ bhāva-samanvitāḥ
Word by word
‘I am the origin of all; from me all proceeds’: thinking thus, the wise, full of earnest conviction, worship me.

Note Śaṅkara: ‘I’ is the supreme Brahman called Vāsudeva. From him alone the whole world goes on through its changes: continuance, destruction, action and the enjoyment of its fruit. The wise are those who have understood the highest truth; bhāva is their absorption in it.

मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम् । कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च ॥ ९ ॥
maccittā madgataprāṇā bodhayantaḥ parasparam | kathayantaśca māṃ nityaṃ tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca || 9 ||
Padaccheda
मद्-चित्ताः मद्-गत-प्राणाः बोधयन्तः परस्परम् कथयन्तः च माम् नित्यम् तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च
mad-cittāḥ mad-gata-prāṇāḥ bodhayantaḥ parasparam kathayantaḥ ca mām nityam tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca
Word by word
With their thought on me, with their lives given to me, enlightening one another and always speaking of me, they are content and rejoice.

Note Śaṅkara: madgataprāṇāḥ: whose organs, the eye and the rest, have gone to the Lord, that is, are withdrawn into him; or whose life is given to him. They rejoice as in the company of one they love.

तेषां सततयुक्तानां भजतां प्रीतिपूर्वकम् । ददामि बुद्धियोगं तं येन मामुपयान्ति ते ॥ १० ॥
teṣāṃ satatayuktānāṃ bhajatāṃ prītipūrvakam | dadāmi buddhiyogaṃ taṃ yena māmupayānti te || 10 ||
Padaccheda
तेषाम् सतत-युक्तानाम् भजताम् प्रीति-पूर्वकम् ददामि बुद्धि-योगम् तम् येन माम् उपयान्ति ते
teṣām satata-yuktānām bhajatām prīti-pūrvakam dadāmi buddhi-yogam tam yena mām upayānti te
Word by word
To them, ever devoted, who worship me with love, I give that yoga of understanding by which they come to me.

Note Śaṅkara: they have turned from all outward seeking and worship him out of love, not for any gain. buddhi is right vision of the truth of the Lord; by union with it they come to know the supreme Lord as their own Self.

तेषामेवानुकम्पार्थमहमज्ञानजं तमः । नाशयाम्यात्मभावस्थो ज्ञानदीपेन भास्वता ॥ ११ ॥
teṣāmevānukampārthamahamajñānajaṃ tamaḥ | nāśayāmyātmabhāvastho jñānadīpena bhāsvatā || 11 ||
Padaccheda
तेषाम् एव अनुकम्पा-अर्थम् अहम् अज्ञान-जम् तमः नाशयामि आत्म-भाव-स्थः ज्ञान-दीपेन भास्वता
teṣām eva anukampā-artham aham ajñāna-jam tamaḥ nāśayāmi ātma-bhāva-sthaḥ jñāna-dīpena bhāsvatā
Word by word
Out of compassion for them alone, I, dwelling in their minds, destroy the darkness born of ignorance with the shining lamp of knowledge.

Note Śaṅkara: the darkness is the delusion that is false notion, born of want of discernment. ātmabhāva is the seat of the mind. The lamp is the discerning thought: fed with the oil of the grace of devotion, fanned by the wind of absorption in the Lord, with a wick of understanding prepared by continence and the like, held in a mind free of passion, sheltered from the winds of attachment and aversion, and shining with the right vision born of constant one-pointed meditation.

अर्जुन उवाचarjuna uvāca
परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान् । पुरुषं शाश्वतं दिव्यमादिदेवमजं विभुम् ॥ १२ ॥
paraṃ brahma paraṃ dhāma pavitraṃ paramaṃ bhavān | puruṣaṃ śāśvataṃ divyamādidevamajaṃ vibhum || 12 ||
Padaccheda
परम् ब्रह्म परम् धाम पवित्रम् परमम् भवान् पुरुषम् शाश्वतम् दिव्यम् आदिदेवम् अजम् विभुम्
param brahma param dhāma pavitram paramam bhavān puruṣam śāśvatam divyam ādidevam ajam vibhum
Word by word
Arjuna said: You are the supreme Brahman, the supreme light, the supreme purifier. The eternal, divine Puruṣa, the first of the gods, unborn and all-pervading:

Note Śaṅkara: paraṃ dhāma is the supreme light. The accusatives go with ‘they call you’ in the next verse.

आहुस्त्वामृषयः सर्वे देवर्षिर्नारदस्तथा । असितो देवलो व्यासः स्वयं चैव ब्रवीषि मे ॥ १३ ॥
āhustvāmṛṣayaḥ sarve devarṣirnāradastathā | asito devalo vyāsaḥ svayaṃ caiva bravīṣi me || 13 ||
Padaccheda
आहुः त्वाम् ऋषयः सर्वे देव-ऋषिः नारदः तथा असितः देवलः व्यासः स्वयम् च एव ब्रवीषि मे
āhuḥ tvām ṛṣayaḥ sarve deva-ṛṣiḥ nāradaḥ tathā asitaḥ devalaḥ vyāsaḥ svayam ca eva bravīṣi me
Word by word
so all the seers call you, and the divine seer Nārada, Asita Devala and Vyāsa; and you yourself tell me so.

Note Śaṅkara: the seers are Vasiṣṭha and the rest.

सर्वमेतदृतं मन्ये यन्मां वदसि केशव । न हि ते भगवन्व्यक्तिं विदुर्देवा न दानवाः ॥ १४ ॥
sarvametadṛtaṃ manye yanmāṃ vadasi keśava | na hi te bhagavanvyaktiṃ vidurdevā na dānavāḥ || 14 ||
Padaccheda
सर्वम् एतत् ऋतम् मन्ये यत् माम् वदसि केशव न हि ते भगवन् व्यक्तिम् विदुः देवाः न दानवाः
sarvam etat ṛtam manye yat mām vadasi keśava na hi te bhagavan vyaktim viduḥ devāḥ na dānavāḥ
Word by word
All this that you say to me I hold to be true, O Keśava; for neither the gods nor the demons, O Lord, know your origin.

Note Śaṅkara glosses vyakti here by prabhava (verse 2).

स्वयमेवात्मनात्मानं वेत्थ त्वं पुरुषोत्तम । भूतभावन भूतेश देवदेव जगत्पते ॥ १५ ॥
svayamevātmanātmānaṃ vettha tvaṃ puruṣottama | bhūtabhāvana bhūteśa devadeva jagatpate || 15 ||
Padaccheda
स्वयम् एव आत्मना आत्मानम् वेत्थ त्वम् पुरुषोत्तम भूतभावन भूतेश देवदेव जगत्पते
svayam eva ātmanā ātmānam vettha tvam puruṣottama bhūtabhāvana bhūteśa devadeva jagatpate
Word by word
You alone know yourself by yourself, O supreme Puruṣa, O source of beings, O Lord of beings, O God of gods, O ruler of the world.

Note Śaṅkara: since he is the source of the gods and the rest, only he knows himself, the Lord of unsurpassed knowledge, lordship and strength.

वक्तुमर्हस्यशेषेण दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः । याभिर्विभूतिभिर्लोकानिमांस्त्वं व्याप्य तिष्ठसि ॥ १६ ॥
vaktumarhasyaśeṣeṇa divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ | yābhirvibhūtibhirlokānimāṃstvaṃ vyāpya tiṣṭhasi || 16 ||
Padaccheda
वक्तुम् अर्हसि अशेषेण दिव्याः हि आत्म-विभूतयः याभिः विभूतिभिः लोकान् इमान् त्वम् व्याप्य तिष्ठसि
vaktum arhasi aśeṣeṇa divyāḥ hi ātma-vibhūtayaḥ yābhiḥ vibhūtibhiḥ lokān imān tvam vyāpya tiṣṭhasi
Word by word
You should tell without remainder your own divine glories, the glories by which you abide pervading these worlds.

Note Śaṅkara: the glories are the wide reaches of his greatness.

कथं विद्यामहं योगिंस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन् । केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्योऽसि भगवन्मया ॥ १७ ॥
kathaṃ vidyāmahaṃ yogiṃstvāṃ sadā paricintayan | keṣu keṣu ca bhāveṣu cintyo 'si bhagavanmayā || 17 ||
Padaccheda
कथम् विद्याम् अहम् योगिन् त्वाम् सदा परिचिन्तयन् केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्यः असि भगवन् मया
katham vidyām aham yogin tvām sadā paricintayan keṣu keṣu ca bhāveṣu cintyaḥ asi bhagavan mayā
Word by word
How may I know you, O yogī, always meditating? And in what various things, O Lord, are you to be thought of by me?
विस्तरेणात्मनो योगं विभूतिं च जनार्दन । भूयः कथय तृप्तिर्हि शृण्वतो नास्ति मेऽमृतम् ॥ १८ ॥
vistareṇātmano yogaṃ vibhūtiṃ ca janārdana | bhūyaḥ kathaya tṛptirhi śṛṇvato nāsti me 'mṛtam || 18 ||
Padaccheda
विस्तरेण आत्मनः योगम् विभूतिम् च जनार्दन भूयः कथय तृप्तिः हि शृण्वतः न अस्ति मे अमृतम्
vistareṇa ātmanaḥ yogam vibhūtim ca janārdana bhūyaḥ kathaya tṛptiḥ hi śṛṇvataḥ na asti me amṛtam
Word by word
Tell again in detail your own power and glory, O Janārdana; for there is no satiety for me as I hear this nectar.

Note Śaṅkara: yoga is the particular power of his lordship; vibhūti the range of things to be meditated on. Janārdana is from ard, ‘to go’: he sends to hell and the like the demons, the people who oppose the gods; or he is the one all people ask for prosperity and the highest good. The nectar is the speech that comes from his mouth.

श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः । प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ नास्त्यन्तो विस्तरस्य मे ॥ १९ ॥
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ | prādhānyataḥ kuruśreṣṭha nāstyanto vistarasya me || 19 ||
Padaccheda
हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्याः हि आत्म-विभूतयः प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ न अस्ति अन्तः विस्तरस्य मे
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi divyāḥ hi ātma-vibhūtayaḥ prādhānyataḥ kuruśreṣṭha na asti antaḥ vistarasya me
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: Come now, I shall tell you my own divine glories, the chief ones, O best of the Kurus; for there is no end to their extent.

Note Śaṅkara: only the chief glory in each kind; all of them could not be told even in a hundred years.

Note divyāḥ … ātmavibhūtayaḥ stand in the nominative though they are the object of kathayiṣyāmi; the text is kept as it stands.

अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः । अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च ॥ २० ॥
ahamātmā guḍākeśa sarvabhūtāśayasthitaḥ | ahamādiśca madhyaṃ ca bhūtānāmanta eva ca || 20 ||
Padaccheda
अहम् आत्मा गुडाकेश सर्व-भूत-आशय-स्थितः अहम् आदिः च मध्यम् च भूतानाम् अन्तः एव च
aham ātmā guḍākeśa sarva-bhūta-āśaya-sthitaḥ aham ādiḥ ca madhyam ca bhūtānām antaḥ eva ca
Word by word
I am the Self, O Guḍākeśa, seated in the heart of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle and the end of beings.

Note Śaṅkara: Guḍākeśa is ‘lord of sleep’, one who has conquered sleep, or ‘thick-haired’. The Lord is always to be meditated on as the inward Self; one who cannot do that should think of him in the things that follow. Beginning, middle and end are origin, continuance and dissolution.

आदित्यानामहं विष्णुर्ज्योतिषां रविरंशुमान् । मरीचिर्मरुतामस्मि नक्षत्राणामहं शशी ॥ २१ ॥
ādityānāmahaṃ viṣṇur jyotiṣāṃ raviraṃśumān | marīcirmarutāmasmi nakṣatrāṇāmahaṃ śaśī || 21 ||
Padaccheda
आदित्यानाम् अहम् विष्णुः ज्योतिषाम् रविः अंशुमान् मरीचिः मरुताम् अस्मि नक्षत्राणाम् अहम् शशी
ādityānām aham viṣṇuḥ jyotiṣām raviḥ aṃśumān marīciḥ marutām asmi nakṣatrāṇām aham śaśī
Word by word
Of the Ādityas I am Viṣṇu; of lights, the radiant sun; I am Marīci of the Maruts; of the stars I am the moon.

Note Śaṅkara: of the twelve Ādityas, the one named Viṣṇu.

वेदानां सामवेदोऽस्मि देवानामस्मि वासवः । इन्द्रियाणां मनश्चास्मि भूतानामस्मि चेतना ॥ २२ ॥
vedānāṃ sāmavedo 'smi devānāmasmi vāsavaḥ | indriyāṇāṃ manaścāsmi bhūtānāmasmi cetanā || 22 ||
Padaccheda
वेदानाम् सामवेदः अस्मि देवानाम् अस्मि वासवः इन्द्रियाणाम् मनः च अस्मि भूतानाम् अस्मि चेतना
vedānām sāmavedaḥ asmi devānām asmi vāsavaḥ indriyāṇām manaḥ ca asmi bhūtānām asmi cetanā
Word by word
Of the Vedas I am the Sāma Veda; of the gods I am Indra; of the senses I am the mind; of beings I am consciousness.

Note Śaṅkara: the senses are eleven. cetanā is the activity of the understanding that is always manifest in the aggregate of body and organs.

रुद्राणां शंकरश्चास्मि वित्तेशो यक्षरक्षसाम् । वसूनां पावकश्चास्मि मेरुः शिखरिणामहम् ॥ २३ ॥
rudrāṇāṃ śaṃkaraścāsmi vitteśo yakṣarakṣasām | vasūnāṃ pāvakaścāsmi meruḥ śikhariṇāmaham || 23 ||
Padaccheda
रुद्राणाम् शंकरः च अस्मि वित्तेशः यक्ष-रक्षसाम् वसूनाम् पावकः च अस्मि मेरुः शिखरिणाम् अहम्
rudrāṇām śaṃkaraḥ ca asmi vitteśaḥ yakṣa-rakṣasām vasūnām pāvakaḥ ca asmi meruḥ śikhariṇām aham
Word by word
Of the Rudras I am Śaṃkara; of the Yakṣas and Rākṣasas, the lord of wealth; of the Vasus I am fire; of peaked mountains I am Meru.

Note Śaṅkara: the Rudras are eleven and the Vasus eight; the lord of wealth is Kubera.

पुरोधसां च मुख्यं मां विद्धि पार्थ बृहस्पतिम् । सेनानीनामहं स्कन्दः सरसामस्मि सागरः ॥ २४ ॥
purodhasāṃ ca mukhyaṃ māṃ viddhi pārtha bṛhaspatim | senānīnām ahaṃ skandaḥ sarasāmasmi sāgaraḥ || 24 ||
Padaccheda
पुरोधसाम् च मुख्यम् माम् विद्धि पार्थ बृहस्पतिम् सेनानीनाम् अहम् स्कन्दः सरसाम् अस्मि सागरः
purodhasām ca mukhyam mām viddhi pārtha bṛhaspatim senānīnām aham skandaḥ sarasām asmi sāgaraḥ
Word by word
And know me, O Pārtha, to be Bṛhaspati, the chief of household priests; of commanders of armies I am Skanda; of lakes I am the ocean.

Note Śaṅkara: Bṛhaspati is the chief because he is the priest of Indra; Skanda is the commander of the army of the gods. The lakes are those dug by the gods.

महर्षीणां भृगुरहं गिरामस्म्येकमक्षरम् । यज्ञानां जपयज्ञोऽस्मि स्थावराणां हिमालयः ॥ २५ ॥
maharṣīṇāṃ bhṛgurahaṃ girāmasmyekamakṣaram | yajñānāṃ japayajño 'smi sthāvarāṇāṃ himālayaḥ || 25 ||
Padaccheda
महा-ऋषीणाम् भृगुः अहम् गिराम् अस्मि एकम् अक्षरम् यज्ञानाम् जप-यज्ञः अस्मि स्थावराणाम् हिमालयः
mahā-ṛṣīṇām bhṛguḥ aham girām asmi ekam akṣaram yajñānām japa-yajñaḥ asmi sthāvarāṇām himālayaḥ
Word by word
Of the great seers I am Bhṛgu; of utterances I am the one syllable; of sacrifices I am the sacrifice of silent repetition; of immovable things, the Himālaya.

Note Śaṅkara: the one syllable is Om.

अश्वत्थः सर्ववृक्षाणां देवर्षीणां च नारदः । गन्धर्वाणां चित्ररथः सिद्धानां कपिलो मुनिः ॥ २६ ॥
aśvatthaḥ sarvavṛkṣāṇāṃ devarṣīṇāṃ ca nāradaḥ | gandharvāṇāṃ citrarathaḥ siddhānāṃ kapilo muniḥ || 26 ||
Padaccheda
अश्वत्थः सर्व-वृक्षाणाम् देव-ऋषीणाम् च नारदः गन्धर्वाणाम् चित्ररथः सिद्धानाम् कपिलः मुनिः
aśvatthaḥ sarva-vṛkṣāṇām deva-ṛṣīṇām ca nāradaḥ gandharvāṇām citrarathaḥ siddhānām kapilaḥ muniḥ
Word by word
Of all trees I am the holy fig tree; of the divine seers, Nārada; of the Gandharvas, Citraratha; of the perfected, the sage Kapila.

Note Śaṅkara: divine seers are gods who became seers by seeing the sacred formulas. The perfected are those who from birth have dharma, knowledge, dispassion and lordship in the highest degree.

उच्चैःश्रवसमश्वानां विद्धि माममृतोद्भवम् । ऐरावतं गजेन्द्राणां नराणां च नराधिपम् ॥ २७ ॥
uccaiḥśravasamaśvānāṃ viddhi māmamṛtodbhavam | airāvataṃ gajendrāṇāṃ narāṇāṃ ca narādhipam || 27 ||
Padaccheda
उच्चैःश्रवसम् अश्वानाम् विद्धि माम् अमृत-उद्भवम् ऐरावतम् गज-इन्द्राणाम् नराणाम् च नराधिपम्
uccaiḥśravasam aśvānām viddhi mām amṛta-udbhavam airāvatam gaja-indrāṇām narāṇām ca narādhipam
Word by word
Of horses know me to be Uccaiḥśravas, born of the nectar; of lordly elephants, Airāvata; and of men, the king.

Note Śaṅkara: born at the churning that was done for the nectar.

आयुधानामहं वज्रं धेनूनामस्मि कामधुक् । प्रजनश्चास्मि कन्दर्पः सर्पाणामस्मि वासुकिः ॥ २८ ॥
āyudhānāmahaṃ vajraṃ dhenūnāmasmi kāmadhuk | prajanaścāsmi kandarpaḥ sarpāṇāmasmi vāsukiḥ || 28 ||
Padaccheda
आयुधानाम् अहम् वज्रम् धेनूनाम् अस्मि कामधुक् प्रजनः च अस्मि कन्दर्पः सर्पाणाम् अस्मि वासुकिः
āyudhānām aham vajram dhenūnām asmi kāmadhuk prajanaḥ ca asmi kandarpaḥ sarpāṇām asmi vāsukiḥ
Word by word
Of weapons I am the thunderbolt; of cows I am the cow that yields every wish; I am the god of love, the begetter; of serpents I am Vāsuki.

Note Śaṅkara: the thunderbolt was made from the bones of Dadhīci. The cow is Vasiṣṭha's, or any cow that yields wishes.

अनन्तश्चास्मि नागानां वरुणो यादसामहम् । पितॄणामर्यमा चास्मि यमः संयमतामहम् ॥ २९ ॥
anantaścāsmi nāgānāṃ varuṇo yādasāmaham | pitṝṇāmaryamā cāsmi yamaḥ saṃyamatāmaham || 29 ||
Padaccheda
अनन्तः च अस्मि नागानाम् वरुणः यादसाम् अहम् पितॄणाम् अर्यमा च अस्मि यमः संयमताम् अहम्
anantaḥ ca asmi nāgānām varuṇaḥ yādasām aham pitṝṇām aryamā ca asmi yamaḥ saṃyamatām aham
Word by word
Of the Nāgas I am Ananta; of the beings of the waters, Varuṇa; of the ancestors I am Aryaman; of those who restrain I am Yama.

Note Śaṅkara: Varuṇa is the king of the deities of the waters, Aryaman the king of the ancestors.

प्रह्लादश्चास्मि दैत्यानां कालः कलयतामहम् । मृगाणां च मृगेन्द्रोऽहं वैनतेयश्च पक्षिणाम् ॥ ३० ॥
prahlādaścāsmi daityānāṃ kālaḥ kalayatāmaham | mṛgāṇāṃ ca mṛgendro 'haṃ vainateyaśca pakṣiṇām || 30 ||
Padaccheda
प्रह्लादः च अस्मि दैत्यानाम् कालः कलयताम् अहम् मृगाणाम् च मृगेन्द्रः अहम् वैनतेयः च पक्षिणाम्
prahlādaḥ ca asmi daityānām kālaḥ kalayatām aham mṛgāṇām ca mṛgendraḥ aham vainateyaḥ ca pakṣiṇām
Word by word
Of the Daityas I am Prahlāda; of those who reckon I am time; of beasts I am the king of beasts; and of birds, the son of Vinatā.

Note Śaṅkara: the king of beasts is the lion, or the tiger; the son of Vinatā is Garuḍa.

पवनः पवतामस्मि रामः शस्त्रभृतामहम् । झषाणां मकरश्चास्मि स्रोतसामस्मि जाह्नवी ॥ ३१ ॥
pavanaḥ pavatāmasmi rāmaḥ śastrabhṛtāmaham | jhaṣāṇāṃ makaraścāsmi srotasāmasmi jāhnavī || 31 ||
Padaccheda
पवनः पवताम् अस्मि रामः शस्त्रभृताम् अहम् झषाणाम् मकरः च अस्मि स्रोतसाम् अस्मि जाह्नवी
pavanaḥ pavatām asmi rāmaḥ śastrabhṛtām aham jhaṣāṇām makaraḥ ca asmi srotasām asmi jāhnavī
Word by word
Of purifiers I am the wind; of those who bear weapons I am Rāma; of fishes I am the makara; of rivers I am the Gaṅgā.

Note Śaṅkara: Rāma is the son of Daśaratha. The makara is a particular kind among fishes and the like.

सर्गाणामादिरन्तश्च मध्यं चैवाहमर्जुन । अध्यात्मविद्या विद्यानां वादः प्रवदतामहम् ॥ ३२ ॥
sargāṇāmādirantaśca madhyaṃ caivāhamarjuna | adhyātmavidyā vidyānāṃ vādaḥ pravadatāmaham || 32 ||
Padaccheda
सर्गाणाम् आदिः अन्तः च मध्यम् च एव अहम् अर्जुन अध्यात्म-विद्या विद्यानाम् वादः प्रवदताम् अहम्
sargāṇām ādiḥ antaḥ ca madhyam ca eva aham arjuna adhyātma-vidyā vidyānām vādaḥ pravadatām aham
Word by word
Of creations I am the beginning, the end and the middle, O Arjuna; of sciences, the science of the Self; of those who debate I am reasoned discussion.

Note Śaṅkara: verse 20 spoke of beings that have a living self; here it is of every creation whatever. The science of the Self is chief because it leads to liberation. vāda is chief among the kinds of debate (vāda, jalpa and vitaṇḍā) because it settles the truth; ‘those who debate’ stands for these kinds.

अक्षराणामकारोऽस्मि द्वंद्वः सामासिकस्य च । अहमेवाक्षयः कालो धाताहं विश्वतोमुखः ॥ ३३ ॥
akṣarāṇāmakāro 'smi dvaṃdvaḥ sāmāsikasya ca | ahamevākṣayaḥ kālo dhātāhaṃ viśvatomukhaḥ || 33 ||
Padaccheda
अक्षराणाम् अकारः अस्मि द्वंद्वः सामासिकस्य च अहम् एव अक्षयः कालः धाता अहम् विश्वतोमुखः
akṣarāṇām akāraḥ asmi dvaṃdvaḥ sāmāsikasya ca aham eva akṣayaḥ kālaḥ dhātā aham viśvatomukhaḥ
Word by word
Of letters I am the letter a, and of the class of compounds the dvandva; I myself am unending time; I am the dispenser who faces everywhere.

Note Śaṅkara: unending time is time as commonly known, made of moments and so on; or the supreme Lord, who is the time even of time. He dispenses the fruit of action to the whole world.

मृत्युः सर्वहरश्चाहमुद्भवश्च भविष्यताम् । कीर्तिः श्रीर्वाक्च नारीणां स्मृतिर्मेधा धृतिः क्षमा ॥ ३४ ॥
mṛtyuḥ sarvaharaścāhamudbhavaśca bhaviṣyatām | kīrtiḥ śrīrvākca nārīṇāṃ smṛtirmedhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā || 34 ||
Padaccheda
मृत्युः सर्व-हरः च अहम् उद्भवः च भविष्यताम् कीर्तिः श्रीः वाक् च नारीणाम् स्मृतिः मेधा धृतिः क्षमा
mṛtyuḥ sarva-haraḥ ca aham udbhavaḥ ca bhaviṣyatām kīrtiḥ śrīḥ vāk ca nārīṇām smṛtiḥ medhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā
Word by word
I am death that carries off all, and the rise of those who are to prosper; and of feminine things I am fame, fortune, speech, memory, intelligence, firmness and forbearance.

Note Śaṅkara: death is of two kinds, that which takes wealth and that which takes life; the second carries off all. Or it is the supreme Lord, who takes all at the dissolution. udbhava is prosperity and the cause of gaining it, for those fit to gain it. Fame and the rest are the best of women; people think themselves fulfilled by even a trace of connection with them.

Note bhaviṣyatām is a future participle; it carries the nearest label.

बृहत्साम तथा साम्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम् । मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः ॥ ३५ ॥
bṛhatsāma tathā sāmnāṃ gāyatrī chandasāmaham | māsānāṃ mārgaśīrṣo 'ham ṛtūnāṃ kusumākaraḥ || 35 ||
Padaccheda
बृहत्साम तथा साम्नाम् गायत्री छन्दसाम् अहम् मासानाम् मार्गशीर्षः अहम् ऋतूनाम् कुसुमाकरः
bṛhatsāma tathā sāmnām gāyatrī chandasām aham māsānām mārgaśīrṣaḥ aham ṛtūnām kusumākaraḥ
Word by word
Likewise of the Sāma chants I am the Bṛhat chant; of metres I am the Gāyatrī; of months I am Mārgaśīrṣa; of seasons, the season of flowers.

Note Śaṅkara: of the verses composed in the Gāyatrī and other metres, the Gāyatrī verse. The season of flowers is spring.

द्यूतं छलयतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् । जयोऽस्मि व्यवसायोऽस्मि सत्त्वं सत्त्ववतामहम् ॥ ३६ ॥
dyūtaṃ chalayatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham | jayo 'smi vyavasāyo 'smi sattvaṃ sattvavatāmaham || 36 ||
Padaccheda
द्यूतम् छलयताम् अस्मि तेजः तेजस्विनाम् अहम् जयः अस्मि व्यवसायः अस्मि सत्त्वम् सत्त्ववताम् अहम्
dyūtam chalayatām asmi tejaḥ tejasvinām aham jayaḥ asmi vyavasāyaḥ asmi sattvam sattvavatām aham
Word by word
Of those who cheat I am gambling; I am the splendour of the splendid; I am victory, I am resolve, I am the sattva of those who have sattva.

Note Śaṅkara: gambling is dicing and the like. Victory is that of the victorious, resolve that of the resolute.

वृष्णीनां वासुदेवोऽस्मि पाण्डवानां धनंजयः । मुनीनामप्यहं व्यासः कवीनामुशना कविः ॥ ३७ ॥
vṛṣṇīnāṃ vāsudevo 'smi pāṇḍavānāṃ dhanaṃjayaḥ | munīnāmapyahaṃ vyāsaḥ kavīnāmuśanā kaviḥ || 37 ||
Padaccheda
वृष्णीनाम् वासुदेवः अस्मि पाण्डवानाम् धनंजयः मुनीनाम् अपि अहम् व्यासः कवीनाम् उशना कविः
vṛṣṇīnām vāsudevaḥ asmi pāṇḍavānām dhanaṃjayaḥ munīnām api aham vyāsaḥ kavīnām uśanā kaviḥ
Word by word
Of the Vṛṣṇis I am Vāsudeva; of the sons of Pāṇḍu, Dhanaṃjaya; of the sages also I am Vyāsa; of the seer-poets, the seer-poet Uśanas.

Note Śaṅkara: Vāsudeva is ‘I myself, your friend’, and Dhanaṃjaya ‘you yourself’. The sages are those given to reflection, who know all things; the kavis are those who see beyond.

दण्डो दमयतामस्मि नीतिरस्मि जिगीषताम् । मौनं चैवास्मि गुह्यानां ज्ञानं ज्ञानवतामहम् ॥ ३८ ॥
daṇḍo damayatāmasmi nītirasmi jigīṣatām | maunaṃ caivāsmi guhyānāṃ jñānaṃ jñānavatāmaham || 38 ||
Padaccheda
दण्डः दमयताम् अस्मि नीतिः अस्मि जिगीषताम् मौनम् च एव अस्मि गुह्यानाम् ज्ञानम् ज्ञानवताम् अहम्
daṇḍaḥ damayatām asmi nītiḥ asmi jigīṣatām maunam ca eva asmi guhyānām jñānam jñānavatām aham
Word by word
Of those who subdue I am the rod; I am the statecraft of those who seek victory; of secrets I am silence; I am the knowledge of those who know.

Note Śaṅkara: the rod is what subdues the unsubdued.

यच्चापि सर्वभूतानां बीजं तदहमर्जुन । न तदस्ति विना यत्स्यान्मया भूतं चराचरम् ॥ ३९ ॥
yaccāpi sarvabhūtānāṃ bījaṃ tadahamarjuna | na tadasti vinā yatsyānmayā bhūtaṃ carācaram || 39 ||
Padaccheda
यत् च अपि सर्व-भूतानाम् बीजम् तत् अहम् अर्जुन न तत् अस्ति विना यत् स्यात् मया भूतम् चराचरम्
yat ca api sarva-bhūtānām bījam tat aham arjuna na tat asti vinā yat syāt mayā bhūtam carācaram
Word by word
And what is the seed of all beings, that also I am, O Arjuna. There is no being, moving or unmoving, that could exist without me.

Note Śaṅkara: this sums up the glories to close the topic. Anything abandoned by the Lord would be without a Self, void; therefore all has him as its Self.

नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परंतप । एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया ॥ ४० ॥
nānto 'sti mama divyānāṃ vibhūtīnāṃ paraṃtapa | eṣa tūddeśataḥ prokto vibhūtervistaro mayā || 40 ||
Padaccheda
न अन्तः अस्ति मम दिव्यानाम् विभूतीनाम् परंतप एषः तु उद्देशतः प्रोक्तः विभूतेः विस्तरः मया
na antaḥ asti mama divyānām vibhūtīnām paraṃtapa eṣaḥ tu uddeśataḥ proktaḥ vibhūteḥ vistaraḥ mayā
Word by word
There is no end to my divine glories, O scorcher of foes; this extent of my glory has been told by me in part only.

Note Śaṅkara: no one can state or know the measure of the divine glories of the Lord, who is the Self of all.

यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा । तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोऽंशसंभवम् ॥ ४१ ॥
yadyadvibhūtimatsattvaṃ śrīmadūrjitameva vā | tattadevāvagaccha tvaṃ mama tejo'ṃśasaṃbhavam || 41 ||
Padaccheda
यत् यत् विभूतिमत् सत्त्वम् श्रीमत् ऊर्जितम् एव वा तत् तत् एव अवगच्छ त्वम् मम तेजः-अंश-संभवम्
yat yat vibhūtimat sattvam śrīmat ūrjitam eva vā tat tat eva avagaccha tvam mama tejaḥ-aṃśa-saṃbhavam
Word by word
Whatever thing has glory, fortune or vigour, know that very thing to have sprung from a portion of my splendour.

Note Śaṅkara: sattva here is a thing. ūrjita: endowed with energy.

अथवा बहुनैतेन किं ज्ञातेन तवार्जुन । विष्टभ्याहमिदं कृत्स्नमेकांशेन स्थितो जगत् ॥ ४२ ॥
athavā bahunaitena kiṃ jñātena tavārjuna | viṣṭabhyāhamidaṃ kṛtsnamekāṃśena sthito jagat || 42 ||
Padaccheda
अथवा बहुना एतेन किम् ज्ञातेन तव अर्जुन विष्टभ्य अहम् इदम् कृत्स्नम् एक-अंशेन स्थितः जगत्
athavā bahunā etena kim jñātena tava arjuna viṣṭabhya aham idam kṛtsnam eka-aṃśena sthitaḥ jagat
Word by word
But what use is it to you, O Arjuna, to know all this at length? I abide, having firmly supported this whole world with a single portion of myself.

Note Śaṅkara: knowing so much still leaves a remainder; hear the matter stated in full. A single portion, one quarter, is the Lord in the form of all beings, as the Veda says: ‘all beings are a quarter of him’.
