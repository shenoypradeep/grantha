सप्तदशोऽध्यायः
Saptadaśo 'dhyāyaḥ · Adhyāya 17 · Śraddhātrayavibhāgayoga
अर्जुन उवाचarjuna uvāca
ये शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः । तेषां निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वमाहो रजस्तमः ॥ १ ॥
ye śāstravidhimutsṛjya yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ | teṣāṃ niṣṭhā tu kā kṛṣṇa sattvamāho rajastamaḥ || 1 ||
Padaccheda
ये शास्त्र-विधिम् उत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धया अन्विताः तेषाम् निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वम् आहो रजः तमः
ye śāstra-vidhim utsṛjya yajante śraddhayā anvitāḥ teṣām niṣṭhā tu kā kṛṣṇa sattvam āho rajaḥ tamaḥ
Word by word
Arjuna said: Those who set aside the rule of scripture and yet worship endowed with faith: what is their standing, O Kṛṣṇa? Is it sattva, or rajas, or tamas?

Bhāṣya The question arises from 16.24. Those meant are people who, not seeing any rule of śruti or smṛti, worship gods and the like with faith merely from seeing what their elders do. Those who know a rule and set it aside are not meant, since such people cannot be said to act with faith. śraddhā is the conviction that it is so (āstikya-buddhi). The question is whether their worship is of sattva, of rajas or of tamas.

श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा । सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां शृणु ॥ २ ॥
trividhā bhavati śraddhā dehināṃ sā svabhāvajā | sāttvikī rājasī caiva tāmasī ceti tāṃ śṛṇu || 2 ||
Padaccheda
त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनाम् सा स्वभाव-जा सात्त्विकी राजसी च एव तामसी च इति ताम् शृणु
trividhā bhavati śraddhā dehinām sā svabhāva-jā sāttvikī rājasī ca eva tāmasī ca iti tām śṛṇu
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: The faith of embodied beings is of three kinds, born of their own nature: of sattva, of rajas and of tamas. Hear of it.

Bhāṣya A general question cannot be answered without distinguishing. svabhāva is the impression of dharma and the like made in other births, which shows itself at the time of death. Faith of sattva has the worship of gods for its object, that of rajas the worship of yakṣas and rākṣasas, that of tamas the worship of ghosts and piśācas.

सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत । श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः ॥ ३ ॥
sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata | śraddhāmayo 'yaṃ puruṣo yo yacchraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ || 3 ||
Padaccheda
सत्त्व-अनुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत श्रद्धा-मयः अयम् पुरुषः यः यत्-श्रद्धः सः एव सः
sattva-anurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata śraddhā-mayaḥ ayam puruṣaḥ yaḥ yat-śraddhaḥ saḥ eva saḥ
Word by word
The faith of everyone is in keeping with his mind, O descendant of Bharata. This man is made of faith: whatever his faith is, that indeed he is.

Bhāṣya Sattva is the inner organ with its particular impressions. puruṣa here is the jīva in the cycle of birth and death; he is mostly faith, and is just as his faith is.

यजन्ते सात्त्विका देवान्यक्षरक्षांसि राजसाः । प्रेतान्भूतगणांश्चान्ये यजन्ते तामसा जनाः ॥ ४ ॥
yajante sāttvikā devānyakṣarakṣāṃsi rājasāḥ | pretānbhūtagaṇāṃścānye yajante tāmasā janāḥ || 4 ||
Padaccheda
यजन्ते सात्त्विकाः देवान् यक्ष-रक्षांसि राजसाः प्रेतान् भूत-गणान् च अन्ये यजन्ते तामसाः जनाः
yajante sāttvikāḥ devān yakṣa-rakṣāṃsi rājasāḥ pretān bhūta-gaṇān ca anye yajante tāmasāḥ janāḥ
Word by word
Those of sattva worship the gods; those of rajas, yakṣas and rākṣasas; the others, men of tamas, worship ghosts and the hosts of spirits.

Bhāṣya So their standing is to be inferred from its effect, the worship. The hosts of spirits include the Seven Mothers and the like.

अशास्त्रविहितं घोरं तप्यन्ते ये तपो जनाः । दम्भाहंकारसंयुक्ताः कामरागबलान्विताः ॥ ५ ॥
aśāstravihitaṃ ghoraṃ tapyante ye tapo janāḥ | dambhāhaṃkārasaṃyuktāḥ kāmarāgabalānvitāḥ || 5 ||
Padaccheda
अशास्त्र-विहितम् घोरम् तप्यन्ते ये तपः जनाः दम्भ-अहंकार-संयुक्ताः काम-राग-बल-अन्विताः
aśāstra-vihitam ghoram tapyante ye tapaḥ janāḥ dambha-ahaṃkāra-saṃyuktāḥ kāma-rāga-bala-anvitāḥ
Word by word
Those men who practise fierce austerity not enjoined by scripture, joined with pretence and the sense of ‘I’, driven by the force of desire and passion,

Bhāṣya Among those who set aside the rule of scripture, one in thousands is of sattva; most are of rajas or tamas. Fierce: causing pain to living beings and to oneself.

कर्शयन्तः शरीरस्थं भूतग्राममचेतसः । मां चैवान्तःशरीरस्थं तान्विद्ध्यासुरनिश्चयान् ॥ ६ ॥
karśayantaḥ śarīrasthaṃ bhūtagrāmamacetasaḥ | māṃ caivāntaḥśarīrasthaṃ tānviddhyāsuraniścayān || 6 ||
Padaccheda
कर्शयन्तः शरीर-स्थम् भूत-ग्रामम् अचेतसः माम् च एव अन्तः-शरीर-स्थम् तान् विद्धि आसुर-निश्चयान्
karśayantaḥ śarīra-stham bhūta-grāmam acetasaḥ mām ca eva antaḥ-śarīra-stham tān viddhi āsura-niścayān
Word by word
undiscerning, wasting the group of organs in the body, and me also who dwell within the body: know them to be of demonic resolve.

Bhāṣya Bhūta-grāma is the collection of organs. ‘Me’ is Nārāyaṇa, the witness of their action and thought; wasting him is not doing as he has taught. They are made known so that they may be avoided.

आहारस्त्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः । यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं शृणु ॥ ७ ॥
āhārastvapi sarvasya trividho bhavati priyaḥ | yajñastapastathā dānaṃ teṣāṃ bhedamimaṃ śṛṇu || 7 ||
Padaccheda
आहारः तु अपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधः भवति प्रियः यज्ञः तपः तथा दानम् तेषाम् भेदम् इमम् शृणु
āhāraḥ tu api sarvasya trividhaḥ bhavati priyaḥ yajñaḥ tapaḥ tathā dānam teṣām bhedam imam śṛṇu
Word by word
The food that is dear to everyone is also of three kinds, and so are sacrifice, austerity and giving. Hear this division of them.

Bhāṣya By one's special liking for one kind of food one may know oneself to be of sattva, rajas or tamas, and so give up the foods that mark rajas and tamas and take those that mark sattva. Sacrifice and the rest are divided likewise, so that those of rajas and tamas may be given up and those of sattva alone practised.

आयुःसत्त्वबलारोग्यसुखप्रीतिविवर्धनाः । रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिरा हृद्या आहाराः सात्त्विकप्रियाः ॥ ८ ॥
āyuḥsattvabalārogyasukhaprītivivardhanāḥ | rasyāḥ snigdhāḥ sthirā hṛdyā āhārāḥ sāttvikapriyāḥ || 8 ||
Padaccheda
आयुः-सत्त्व-बल-आरोग्य-सुख-प्रीति-विवर्धनाः रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिराः हृद्याः आहाराः सात्त्विक-प्रियाः
āyuḥ-sattva-bala-ārogya-sukha-prīti-vivardhanāḥ rasyāḥ snigdhāḥ sthirāḥ hṛdyāḥ āhārāḥ sāttvika-priyāḥ
Word by word
Foods that increase life, vigour, strength, health, happiness and delight, that are savoury, rich in oil, lasting and pleasing to the heart, are dear to one of sattva.

Bhāṣya Lasting: staying long in the body.

कट्वम्ललवणात्युष्णतीक्ष्णरूक्षविदाहिनः । आहारा राजसस्येष्टा दुःखशोकामयप्रदाः ॥ ९ ॥
kaṭvamlalavaṇātyuṣṇatīkṣṇarūkṣavidāhinaḥ | āhārā rājasasyeṣṭā duḥkhaśokāmayapradāḥ || 9 ||
Padaccheda
कटु-अम्ल-लवण-अत्युष्ण-तीक्ष्ण-रूक्ष-विदाहिनः आहाराः राजसस्य इष्टाः दुःख-शोक-आमय-प्रदाः
kaṭu-amla-lavaṇa-atyuṣṇa-tīkṣṇa-rūkṣa-vidāhinaḥ āhārāḥ rājasasya iṣṭāḥ duḥkha-śoka-āmaya-pradāḥ
Word by word
Foods that are bitter, sour, salty, very hot, pungent, dry and burning, that give pain, grief and sickness, are liked by one of rajas.

Bhāṣya Ati, ‘very’, is to be joined with each: very bitter, very pungent and so on.

यातयामं गतरसं पूति पर्युषितं च यत् । उच्छिष्टमपि चामेध्यं भोजनं तामसप्रियम् ॥ १० ॥
yātayāmaṃ gatarasaṃ pūti paryuṣitaṃ ca yat | ucchiṣṭamapi cāmedhyaṃ bhojanaṃ tāmasapriyam || 10 ||
Padaccheda
यातयामम् गत-रसम् पूति पर्युषितम् च यत् उच्छिष्टम् अपि च अमेध्यम् भोजनम् तामस-प्रियम्
yātayāmam gata-rasam pūti paryuṣitam ca yat ucchiṣṭam api ca amedhyam bhojanam tāmasa-priyam
Word by word
Food that is half-cooked, tasteless, putrid, stale, left over and unfit for sacrifice is dear to one of tamas.

Bhāṣya Yātayāma is ‘not fully cooked’, since food that has lost its strength is meant by gata-rasa. Stale: cooked and kept overnight. Left over: what remains after eating.

अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते । यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः ॥ ११ ॥
aphalākāṅkṣibhiryajño vidhidṛṣṭo ya ijyate | yaṣṭavyameveti manaḥ samādhāya sa sāttvikaḥ || 11 ||
Padaccheda
अफल-आकाङ्क्षिभिः यज्ञः विधि-दृष्टः यः इज्यते यष्टव्यम् एव इति मनः समाधाय सः सात्त्विकः
aphala-ākāṅkṣibhiḥ yajñaḥ vidhi-dṛṣṭaḥ yaḥ ijyate yaṣṭavyam eva iti manaḥ samādhāya saḥ sāttvikaḥ
Word by word
The sacrifice that is offered, as seen in the rule, by those who do not long for its fruit, having settled the mind that sacrifice is simply to be done, is of sattva.

Bhāṣya Resolved that only the carrying out of the sacrifice itself is to be done, and that no end of their own is to be gained by it.

अभिसंधाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत् । इज्यते भरतश्रेष्ठ तं यज्ञं विद्धि राजसम् ॥ १२ ॥
abhisaṃdhāya tu phalaṃ dambhārthamapi caiva yat | ijyate bharataśreṣṭha taṃ yajñaṃ viddhi rājasam || 12 ||
Padaccheda
अभिसंधाय तु फलम् दम्भ-अर्थम् अपि च एव यत् इज्यते भरत-श्रेष्ठ तम् यज्ञम् विद्धि राजसम्
abhisaṃdhāya tu phalam dambha-artham api ca eva yat ijyate bharata-śreṣṭha tam yajñam viddhi rājasam
Word by word
But that which is offered aiming at the fruit, and also for the sake of show, O best of the Bharatas: know that sacrifice to be of rajas.
विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम् । श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते ॥ १३ ॥
vidhihīnamasṛṣṭānnaṃ mantrahīnamadakṣiṇam | śraddhāvirahitaṃ yajñaṃ tāmasaṃ paricakṣate || 13 ||
Padaccheda
विधि-हीनम् असृष्ट-अन्नम् मन्त्र-हीनम् अदक्षिणम् श्रद्धा-विरहितम् यज्ञम् तामसम् परिचक्षते
vidhi-hīnam asṛṣṭa-annam mantra-hīnam adakṣiṇam śraddhā-virahitam yajñam tāmasam paricakṣate
Word by word
The sacrifice that is without the rule, in which no food is given away, without mantras, without fees to the priests and devoid of faith, they call of tamas.

Bhāṣya The food is that given to brāhmaṇas. ‘Without mantras’: wanting in mantra, accent or syllable.

देवद्विजगुरुप्राज्ञपूजनं शौचमार्जवम् । ब्रह्मचर्यमहिंसा च शारीरं तप उच्यते ॥ १४ ॥
devadvijaguruprājñapūjanaṃ śaucamārjavam | brahmacaryamahiṃsā ca śārīraṃ tapa ucyate || 14 ||
Padaccheda
देव-द्विज-गुरु-प्राज्ञ-पूजनम् शौचम् आर्जवम् ब्रह्मचर्यम् अहिंसा च शारीरम् तपः उच्यते
deva-dvija-guru-prājña-pūjanam śaucam ārjavam brahmacaryam ahiṃsā ca śārīram tapaḥ ucyate
Word by word
Worship of the gods, the twice-born, teachers and the wise, purity, uprightness, continence and non-injury are called austerity of the body.

Bhāṣya ‘of the body’: carried out by all the factors of action, the agent and the rest, with the body as chief among them (18.15).

अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत् । स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्मयं तप उच्यते ॥ १५ ॥
anudvegakaraṃ vākyaṃ satyaṃ priyahitaṃ ca yat | svādhyāyābhyasanaṃ caiva vāṅmayaṃ tapa ucyate || 15 ||
Padaccheda
अनुद्वेग-करम् वाक्यम् सत्यम् प्रिय-हितम् च यत् स्वाध्याय-अभ्यसनम् च एव वाङ्मयम् तपः उच्यते
anudvega-karam vākyam satyam priya-hitam ca yat svādhyāya-abhyasanam ca eva vāṅmayam tapaḥ ucyate
Word by word
Speech that causes no distress, that is true, pleasant and beneficial, and the practice of reciting the Veda, are called austerity of speech.

Bhāṣya All four qualities must be there together; speech meant to convince another that lacks one, two or three of them is not austerity of speech. An example: ‘Be calm, my child; practise recitation and yoga, and so it will be well with you.’

मनःप्रसादः सौम्यत्वं मौनमात्मविनिग्रहः । भावसंशुद्धिरित्येतत्तपो मानसमुच्यते ॥ १६ ॥
manaḥprasādaḥ saumyatvaṃ maunamātmavinigrahaḥ | bhāvasaṃśuddhirityetattapo mānasamucyate || 16 ||
Padaccheda
मनः-प्रसादः सौम्यत्वम् मौनम् आत्म-विनिग्रहः भाव-संशुद्धिः इति एतत् तपः मानसम् उच्यते
manaḥ-prasādaḥ saumyatvam maunam ātma-vinigrahaḥ bhāva-saṃśuddhiḥ iti etat tapaḥ mānasam ucyate
Word by word
Serenity of mind, kindliness, silence, restraint of the mind and purity of intent: this is called austerity of the mind.

Bhāṣya Saumyatva is what is called good-heartedness, a state of the inner organ inferred from the brightness of the face and the like. Restraint of speech too comes from control of the mind, so the cause is named by its effect: silence is the mind's control in regard to speech, while ātma-vinigraha is restraint of the mind in general. Purity of intent is absence of deceit in dealing with others.

श्रद्धया परया तप्तं तपस्तत्त्रिविधं नरैः । अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्युक्तैः सात्त्विकं परिचक्षते ॥ १७ ॥
śraddhayā parayā taptaṃ tapastattrividhaṃ naraiḥ | aphalākāṅkṣibhiryuktaiḥ sāttvikaṃ paricakṣate || 17 ||
Padaccheda
श्रद्धया परया तप्तम् तपः तत् त्रिविधम् नरैः अफल-आकाङ्क्षिभिः युक्तैः सात्त्विकम् परिचक्षते
śraddhayā parayā taptam tapaḥ tat trividham naraiḥ aphala-ākāṅkṣibhiḥ yuktaiḥ sāttvikam paricakṣate
Word by word
That threefold austerity, practised with supreme faith by men who are collected and do not long for its fruit, they call of sattva.

Bhāṣya Threefold: having three seats, body, speech and mind.

सत्कारमानपूजार्थं तपो दम्भेन चैव यत् । क्रियते तदिह प्रोक्तं राजसं चलमध्रुवम् ॥ १८ ॥
satkāramānapūjārthaṃ tapo dambhena caiva yat | kriyate tadiha proktaṃ rājasaṃ calamadhruvam || 18 ||
Padaccheda
सत्कार-मान-पूजा-अर्थम् तपः दम्भेन च एव यत् क्रियते तत् इह प्रोक्तम् राजसम् चलम् अध्रुवम्
satkāra-māna-pūjā-artham tapaḥ dambhena ca eva yat kriyate tat iha proktam rājasam calam adhruvam
Word by word
The austerity that is done for the sake of praise, honour and worship, and with pretence, is said here to be of rajas, unsteady and uncertain.

Bhāṣya Praise is being called ‘a good brāhmaṇa, an ascetic’; honour is rising to greet, saluting and the like; worship is washing of the feet, offerings and feeding. Unsteady: its fruit comes only at times.

मूढग्राहेणात्मनो यत्पीडया क्रियते तपः । परस्योत्सादनार्थं वा तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १९ ॥
mūḍhagrāheṇātmano yatpīḍayā kriyate tapaḥ | parasyotsādanārthaṃ vā tattāmasamudāhṛtam || 19 ||
Padaccheda
मूढ-ग्राहेण आत्मनः यत् पीडया क्रियते तपः परस्य उत्सादन-अर्थम् वा तत् तामसम् उदाहृतम्
mūḍha-grāheṇa ātmanaḥ yat pīḍayā kriyate tapaḥ parasya utsādana-artham vā tat tāmasam udāhṛtam
Word by word
The austerity that is done from a deluded notion, with torture of oneself, or for the destruction of another, is declared to be of tamas.
दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे । देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥ २० ॥
dātavyamiti yaddānaṃ dīyate 'nupakāriṇe | deśe kāle ca pātre ca taddānaṃ sāttvikaṃ smṛtam || 20 ||
Padaccheda
दातव्यम् इति यत् दानम् दीयते अनुपकारिणे देशे काले च पात्रे च तत् दानम् सात्त्विकम् स्मृतम्
dātavyam iti yat dānam dīyate anupakāriṇe deśe kāle ca pātre ca tat dānam sāttvikam smṛtam
Word by word
The gift that is given because it ought to be given, to one who does no service in return, in the right place, at the right time and to a worthy person, is held to be of sattva.

Bhāṣya To one unable to make a return; or, if able, without looking for one. The place is a holy one such as Kurukṣetra; the time, the sun's passage into a new sign and the like; the worthy person, one who has mastered the Veda with its six auxiliaries.

यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः । दीयते च परिक्लिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम् ॥ २१ ॥
yattu pratyupakārārthaṃ phalamuddiśya vā punaḥ | dīyate ca parikliṣṭaṃ taddānaṃ rājasaṃ smṛtam || 21 ||
Padaccheda
यत् तु प्रत्युपकार-अर्थम् फलम् उद्दिश्य वा पुनः दीयते च परिक्लिष्टम् तत् दानम् राजसम् स्मृतम्
yat tu pratyupakāra-artham phalam uddiśya vā punaḥ dīyate ca parikliṣṭam tat dānam rājasam smṛtam
Word by word
But that which is given for the sake of a return, or again with its fruit in view, and grudgingly, is held to be a gift of rajas.

Bhāṣya ‘in time he will do me a service in return’; or ‘the unseen fruit of this gift will be mine’. parikliṣṭa: given with distress.

अदेशकाले यद्दानमपात्रेभ्यश्च दीयते । असत्कृतमवज्ञातं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ २२ ॥
adeśakāle yaddānam apātrebhyaśca dīyate | asatkṛtamavajñātaṃ tattāmasamudāhṛtam || 22 ||
Padaccheda
अदेश-काले यत् दानम् अपात्रेभ्यः च दीयते असत्कृतम् अवज्ञातम् तत् तामसम् उदाहृतम्
adeśa-kāle yat dānam apātrebhyaḥ ca dīyate asatkṛtam avajñātam tat tāmasam udāhṛtam
Word by word
The gift that is given at the wrong place and time and to unworthy persons, without respect and with contempt, is declared to be of tamas.

Bhāṣya Unworthy persons are fools, thieves and the like. Or, even when place, time and person are right, a gift given without kind words, washing of the feet and honour, and with contempt for the receiver.

ॐ तत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः । ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा ॥ २३ ॥
oṃ tatsaditi nirdeśo brahmaṇastrividhaḥ smṛtaḥ | brāhmaṇāstena vedāśca yajñāśca vihitāḥ purā || 23 ||
Padaccheda
ॐ तत् सत् इति निर्देशः ब्रह्मणः त्रिविधः स्मृतः ब्राह्मणाः तेन वेदाः च यज्ञाः च विहिताः पुरा
om tat sat iti nirdeśaḥ brahmaṇaḥ trividhaḥ smṛtaḥ brāhmaṇāḥ tena vedāḥ ca yajñāḥ ca vihitāḥ purā
Word by word
‘Om tat sat’: this is held to be the threefold designation of Brahman. By it the brāhmaṇas, the Vedas and the sacrifices were made of old.

Bhāṣya This teaching is given to make sacrifice, giving, austerity and the rest complete. nirdeśa is that by which a thing is pointed out, a name; it is so thought of in the Upaniṣads by the knowers of Brahman. The second half is said in praise of the designation.

तस्मादोमित्युदाहृत्य यज्ञदानतपःक्रियाः । प्रवर्तन्ते विधानोक्ताः सततं ब्रह्मवादिनाम् ॥ २४ ॥
tasmādomityudāhṛtya yajñadānatapaḥkriyāḥ | pravartante vidhānoktāḥ satataṃ brahmavādinām || 24 ||
Padaccheda
तस्मात् ॐ इति उदाहृत्य यज्ञ-दान-तपः-क्रियाः प्रवर्तन्ते विधान-उक्ताः सततम् ब्रह्म-वादिनाम्
tasmāt om iti udāhṛtya yajña-dāna-tapaḥ-kriyāḥ pravartante vidhāna-uktāḥ satatam brahma-vādinām
Word by word
Therefore the acts of sacrifice, giving and austerity laid down by the rule are always begun, by those who expound the Veda, after uttering ‘Om’.

Bhāṣya Laid down by the rule: enjoined by scripture. brahma-vādin: given to uttering brahman.

तदित्यनभिसंधाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः । दानक्रियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥ २५ ॥
tadityanabhisaṃdhāya phalaṃ yajñatapaḥkriyāḥ | dānakriyāśca vividhāḥ kriyante mokṣakāṅkṣibhiḥ || 25 ||
Padaccheda
तत् इति अनभिसंधाय फलम् यज्ञ-तपः-क्रियाः दान-क्रियाः च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्ष-काङ्क्षिभिः
tat iti anabhisaṃdhāya phalam yajña-tapaḥ-kriyāḥ dāna-kriyāḥ ca vividhāḥ kriyante mokṣa-kāṅkṣibhiḥ
Word by word
With ‘tat’, and without aiming at the fruit, the acts of sacrifice and austerity and the various acts of giving are done by those who seek liberation.

Bhāṣya After uttering tat, a name of Brahman. The acts of giving are gifts of land, gold and the like.

सद्भावे साधुभावे च सदित्येतत्प्रयुज्यते । प्रशस्ते कर्मणि तथा सच्छब्दः पार्थ युज्यते ॥ २६ ॥
sadbhāve sādhubhāve ca sadityetatprayujyate | praśaste karmaṇi tathā sacchabdaḥ pārtha yujyate || 26 ||
Padaccheda
सद्भावे साधु-भावे च सत् इति एतत् प्रयुज्यते प्रशस्ते कर्मणि तथा सत्-शब्दः पार्थ युज्यते
sadbhāve sādhu-bhāve ca sat iti etat prayujyate praśaste karmaṇi tathā sat-śabdaḥ pārtha yujyate
Word by word
This word ‘sat’ is used for coming into being and for goodness; and likewise, O Pārtha, the word ‘sat’ is used for an auspicious act.

Bhāṣya Sad-bhāva is the coming into being of what was not, as the birth of a son; sādhu-bhāva is a man of bad conduct becoming one of good conduct. An auspicious act is marriage and the like. sat is a name of Brahman.

यज्ञे तपसि दाने च स्थितिः सदिति चोच्यते । कर्म चैव तदर्थीयं सदित्येवाभिधीयते ॥ २७ ॥
yajñe tapasi dāne ca sthitiḥ saditi cocyate | karma caiva tadarthīyaṃ sadityevābhidhīyate || 27 ||
Padaccheda
यज्ञे तपसि दाने च स्थितिः सत् इति च उच्यते कर्म च एव तत्-अर्थीयम् सत् इति एव अभिधीयते
yajñe tapasi dāne ca sthitiḥ sat iti ca ucyate karma ca eva tat-arthīyam sat iti eva abhidhīyate
Word by word
Steadfastness in sacrifice, in austerity and in giving is also called ‘sat’; and action for the sake of these is indeed named ‘sat’.

Bhāṣya Tad-arthīya: for the sake of sacrifice, giving and austerity; or for the sake of him whose three names are the topic, the Lord. So an act of sacrifice, giving or austerity, even if not of sattva and imperfect, when done with faith and with the use of the three names of Brahman, is made complete and of sattva.

अश्रद्धया हुतं दत्तं तपस्तप्तं कृतं च यत् । असदित्युच्यते पार्थ न च तत्प्रेत्य नो इह ॥ २८ ॥
aśraddhayā hutaṃ dattaṃ tapastaptaṃ kṛtaṃ ca yat | asadityucyate pārtha na ca tatpretya no iha || 28 ||
Padaccheda
अश्रद्धया हुतम् दत्तम् तपः तप्तम् कृतम् च यत् असत् इति उच्यते पार्थ न च तत् प्रेत्य नो इह
aśraddhayā hutam dattam tapaḥ taptam kṛtam ca yat asat iti ucyate pārtha na ca tat pretya no iha
Word by word
Whatever is offered in the fire, given, practised as austerity or done without faith is called ‘asat’, O Pārtha; it is of no avail after death, nor here.

Bhāṣya Since everything is made complete chiefly by faith. ‘Done’: praise, salutation and the like. It is asat because it lies outside the path that is the means of reaching me. Though it costs much effort, it bears no fruit hereafter; nor here, since the good censure it.
