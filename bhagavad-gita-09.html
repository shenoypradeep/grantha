नवमोऽध्यायः
Navamo 'dhyāyaḥ · Adhyāya 9 · Rājavidyārājaguhyayoga
श्रीभगवानुवाचśrībhagavān uvāca
इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे । ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥ १ ॥
idaṃ tu te guhyatamaṃ pravakṣyāmyanasūyave | jñānaṃ vijñānasahitaṃ yajjñātvā mokṣyase 'śubhāt || 1 ||
Padaccheda
इदम् तु ते गुह्यतमम् प्रवक्ष्यामि अनसूयवे ज्ञानम् विज्ञान-सहितम् यत् ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसे अशुभात्
idam tu te guhyatamam pravakṣyāmi anasūyave jñānam vijñāna-sahitam yat jñātvā mokṣyase aśubhāt
Word by word
The Blessed Lord said: To you, who do not find fault, I shall declare this most secret knowledge, together with realization, knowing which you will be freed from evil.

Bhāṣya ‘this’ is the knowledge of Brahman, taught in the earlier adhyāyas and now to be taught. The word tu marks it out: this right knowledge alone, ‘Vāsudeva is all’, ‘all this is the Self’, ‘one only, without a second’, is the direct means to liberation, and nothing else. Realization is experience. The evil is the bondage of the cycle of birth and death.

राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् । प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम् ॥ २ ॥
rājavidyā rājaguhyaṃ pavitramidamuttamam | pratyakṣāvagamaṃ dharmyaṃ susukhaṃ kartumavyayam || 2 ||
Padaccheda
राज-विद्या राज-गुह्यम् पवित्रम् इदम् उत्तमम् प्रत्यक्ष-अवगमम् धर्म्यम् सु-सुखम् कर्तुम् अव्ययम्
rāja-vidyā rāja-guhyam pavitram idam uttamam pratyakṣa-avagamam dharmyam su-sukham kartum avyayam
Word by word
This is the king of sciences, the king of secrets, the supreme purifier; it is known by direct experience, in accord with dharma, very easy to practise, and imperishable.

Bhāṣya The knowledge of Brahman shines above all sciences. It burns up in a moment, root and all, the action of merit and demerit stored over many thousands of births. It is experienced directly, as pleasure is. It is easy, like the knowledge that tells one gem from another; and though easy, its fruit does not waste away as that of action does.

अश्रद्दधानाः पुरुषा धर्मस्यास्य परंतप । अप्राप्य मां निवर्तन्ते मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि ॥ ३ ॥
aśraddadhānāḥ puruṣā dharmasyāsya paraṃtapa | aprāpya māṃ nivartante mṛtyusaṃsāravartmani || 3 ||
Padaccheda
अ-श्रद्दधानाः पुरुषाः धर्मस्य अस्य परंतप अ-प्राप्य माम् निवर्तन्ते मृत्यु-संसार-वर्त्मनि
a-śraddadhānāḥ puruṣāḥ dharmasya asya paraṃtapa a-prāpya mām nivartante mṛtyu-saṃsāra-vartmani
Word by word
Men who have no faith in this dharma, O scorcher of foes, without reaching me, remain on the path of the cycle of birth and death.

Bhāṣya The dharma is the knowledge of the Self; they believe neither in its nature nor in its fruit, and hold the doctrine of the demons that the body alone is the Self. They do not gain even devotion, one of the paths to the Lord. nivartante he glosses as ‘assuredly remain’, on the path that leads to hell, animal birth and the like.

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना । मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः ॥ ४ ॥
mayā tatamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagadavyaktamūrtinā | matsthāni sarvabhūtāni na cāhaṃ teṣvavasthitaḥ || 4 ||
Padaccheda
मया ततम् इदम् सर्वम् जगत् अव्यक्त-मूर्तिना मद्-स्थानि सर्व-भूतानि न च अहम् तेषु अवस्थितः
mayā tatam idam sarvam jagat avyakta-mūrtinā mad-sthāni sarva-bhūtāni na ca aham teṣu avasthitaḥ
Word by word
This whole world is pervaded by me, whose form is unmanifest. All beings abide in me, and I do not abide in them.

Bhāṣya By the Lord's higher nature, which is beyond the reach of the senses. All beings, from Brahmā down to a tuft of grass, abide in him, since nothing without a Self can exist or act; they have their being through him as their Self. To the dull-witted he therefore seems to be located in them; but he is not, for having no contact as a bodily thing has, he is more inward even than space, and what has no contact is not contained in anything.

न च मत्स्थानि भूतानि पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् । भूतभृन्न च भूतस्थो ममात्मा भूतभावनः ॥ ५ ॥
na ca matsthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogamaiśvaram | bhūtabhṛnna ca bhūtastho mamātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ || 5 ||
Padaccheda
न च मद्-स्थानि भूतानि पश्य मे योगम् ऐश्वरम् भूत-भृत् न च भूत-स्थः मम आत्मा भूत-भावनः
na ca mad-sthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogam aiśvaram bhūta-bhṛt na ca bhūta-sthaḥ mama ātmā bhūta-bhāvanaḥ
Word by word
And yet beings do not abide in me: behold my divine power. My Self sustains beings and brings beings into being, yet does not abide in beings.

Bhāṣya Beings do not abide in him because he has no contact with anything; the Veda says ‘unattached, for he does not attach himself’. yoga here is the Lord's way of being, the true nature of the Self. ‘My Self’ follows ordinary usage, which separates the aggregate of the body and lays the sense of ‘I’ on it; it does not mean that the Self has another Self.

यथाकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान् । तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपधारय ॥ ६ ॥
yathākāśasthito nityaṃ vāyuḥ sarvatrago mahān | tathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni matsthānītyupadhāraya || 6 ||
Padaccheda
यथा आकाश-स्थितः नित्यम् वायुः सर्वत्रगः महान् तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मद्-स्थानि इति उपधारय
yathā ākāśa-sthitaḥ nityam vāyuḥ sarvatragaḥ mahān tathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni mad-sthāni iti upadhāraya
Word by word
As the mighty wind, moving everywhere, always rests in space, so all beings abide in me: understand it thus.

Bhāṣya This illustrates the two preceding verses. Beings rest in the Lord, who is all-pervading like space, without any contact.

सर्वभूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिं यान्ति मामिकाम् । कल्पक्षये पुनस्तानि कल्पादौ विसृजाम्यहम् ॥ ७ ॥
sarvabhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtiṃ yānti māmikām | kalpakṣaye punastāni kalpādau visṛjāmyaham || 7 ||
Padaccheda
सर्व-भूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिम् यान्ति मामिकाम् कल्प-क्षये पुनः तानि कल्प-आदौ विसृजामि अहम्
sarva-bhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtim yānti māmikām kalpa-kṣaye punaḥ tāni kalpa-ādau visṛjāmi aham
Word by word
All beings, O son of Kuntī, go to my Prakṛti at the end of an age; at the beginning of an age I send them forth again.

Bhāṣya This Prakṛti is the lower one, made of the three guṇas.

प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः । भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात् ॥ ८ ॥
prakṛtiṃ svāmavaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ | bhūtagrāmamimaṃ kṛtsnamavaśaṃ prakṛtervaśāt || 8 ||
Padaccheda
प्रकृतिम् स्वाम् अवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः भूत-ग्रामम् इमम् कृत्स्नम् अवशम् प्रकृतेः वशात्
prakṛtim svām avaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ bhūta-grāmam imam kṛtsnam avaśam prakṛteḥ vaśāt
Word by word
Having taken control of my own Prakṛti, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, helpless under the sway of Prakṛti.

Bhāṣya This Prakṛti is ignorance. The multitude of beings is not free: it is made subject to another by ignorance and the other faults.

न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनंजय । उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु ॥ ९ ॥
na ca māṃ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanaṃjaya | udāsīnavad āsīnam asaktaṃ teṣu karmasu || 9 ||
Padaccheda
न च माम् तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनंजय उदासीनवत् आसीनम् अ-सक्तम् तेषु कर्मसु
na ca mām tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanaṃjaya udāsīnavat āsīnam a-saktam teṣu karmasu
Word by word
And those actions do not bind me, O Dhanaṃjaya, who sit like one unconcerned, unattached to those actions.

Bhāṣya This answers the doubt that the Lord, in making beings unequal, would be touched by merit and demerit. He sits as an onlooker, because the Self does not change, without attachment to the fruit and without the notion ‘I do’. For anyone else too, the absence of these is what frees from action; otherwise the fool is bound by his actions like a silkworm.

मयाध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् । हेतुनानेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते ॥ १० ॥
mayādhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sacarācaram | hetunānena kaunteya jagadviparivartate || 10 ||
Padaccheda
मया अध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् हेतुना अनेन कौन्तेय जगत् विपरिवर्तते
mayā adhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sacarācaram hetunā anena kaunteya jagat viparivartate
Word by word
With me as overseer, Prakṛti brings forth the moving and the unmoving; for this reason, O son of Kuntī, the world revolves.

Bhāṣya This removes the seeming contradiction between ‘I send forth’ and ‘sitting unconcerned’. The Lord is the overseer as the changeless Self whose nature is pure seeing; his māyā, Prakṛti made of the three guṇas and of the nature of ignorance, produces the world. All the world's activity has its ground in becoming an object of that seeing. Since in truth there is no other conscious enjoyer, the question ‘for what purpose is this creation?’ and its answer do not arise, as the Veda says: ‘Who truly knows? Who here can declare it?’

अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम् । परं भावमजानन्तो मम भूतमहेश्वरम् ॥ ११ ॥
avajānanti māṃ mūḍhā mānuṣīṃ tanumāśritam | paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mama bhūtamaheśvaram || 11 ||
Padaccheda
अवजानन्ति माम् मूढाः मानुषीम् तनुम् आश्रितम् परम् भावम् अ-जानन्तः मम भूत-महेश्वरम्
avajānanti mām mūḍhāḥ mānuṣīm tanum āśritam param bhāvam a-jānantaḥ mama bhūta-maheśvaram
Word by word
The deluded disregard me, who have taken a human body, not knowing my higher nature as the great Lord of beings.

Bhāṣya He acts by means of a human body. His higher nature is the reality of the supreme Self, like space and more inward than space; he is their own Self. By their contempt for him these wretches are ruined.

मोघाशा मोघकर्माणो मोघज्ञाना विचेतसः । राक्षसीमासुरीं चैव प्रकृतिं मोहिनीं श्रिताः ॥ १२ ॥
moghāśā moghakarmāṇo moghajñānā vicetasaḥ | rākṣasīmāsurīṃ caiva prakṛtiṃ mohinīṃ śritāḥ || 12 ||
Padaccheda
मोघ-आशाः मोघ-कर्माणः मोघ-ज्ञानाः विचेतसः राक्षसीम् आसुरीम् च एव प्रकृतिम् मोहिनीम् श्रिताः
mogha-āśāḥ mogha-karmāṇaḥ mogha-jñānāḥ vicetasaḥ rākṣasīm āsurīm ca eva prakṛtim mohinīm śritāḥ
Word by word
Of vain hopes, vain actions and vain knowledge, without discernment, they have resorted to the deluding nature of fiends and demons.

Bhāṣya The rites they perform, such as the Agnihotra, are fruitless because they slight the Lord, their own Self. That nature holds the body to be the Self; they are given to saying ‘cut, split, drink, eat, seize another's goods’ and to cruel deeds.

महात्मानस्तु मां पार्थ दैवीं प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः । भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो ज्ञात्वा भूतादिमव्ययम् ॥ १३ ॥
mahātmānastu māṃ pārtha daivīṃ prakṛtimāśritāḥ | bhajantyananyamanaso jñātvā bhūtādimavyayam || 13 ||
Padaccheda
महात्मानः तु माम् पार्थ दैवीम् प्रकृतिम् आश्रिताः भजन्ति अन्-अन्य-मनसः ज्ञात्वा भूत-आदिम् अव्ययम्
mahātmānaḥ tu mām pārtha daivīm prakṛtim āśritāḥ bhajanti an-anya-manasaḥ jñātvā bhūta-ādim avyayam
Word by word
But the great souls, O Pārtha, resorting to the divine nature, worship me with mind on nothing else, knowing me as the imperishable origin of beings.

Bhāṣya These have faith and have set out on the path to liberation that is devotion to the Lord. Great souls: not small-minded. The nature of the gods is marked by calm, self-control, compassion, faith and the like. He is the cause of the elements, space and the rest, and of living beings.

सततं कीर्तयन्तो मां यतन्तश्च दृढव्रताः । नमस्यन्तश्च मां भक्त्या नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ॥ १४ ॥
satataṃ kīrtayanto māṃ yatantaśca dṛḍhavratāḥ | namasyantaśca māṃ bhaktyā nityayuktā upāsate || 14 ||
Padaccheda
सततम् कीर्तयन्तः माम् यतन्तः च दृढ-व्रताः नमस्यन्तः च माम् भक्त्या नित्य-युक्ताः उपासते
satatam kīrtayantaḥ mām yatantaḥ ca dṛḍha-vratāḥ namasyantaḥ ca mām bhaktyā nitya-yuktāḥ upāsate
Word by word
Always glorifying me, striving, firm in their vows, and bowing to me with devotion, ever collected, they worship me.

Bhāṣya They glorify the Lord, whose nature is Brahman; they strive by withdrawing the senses, by calm, self-control, compassion, non-injury and the like; and they bow to him as the Self that dwells in the heart.

ज्ञानयज्ञेन चाप्यन्ये यजन्तो मामुपासते । एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुधा विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ १५ ॥
jñānayajñena cāpyanye yajanto māmupāsate | ekatvena pṛthaktvena bahudhā viśvatomukham || 15 ||
Padaccheda
ज्ञान-यज्ञेन च अपि अन्ये यजन्तः माम् उपासते एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुधा विश्वतोमुखम्
jñāna-yajñena ca api anye yajantaḥ mām upāsate ekatvena pṛthaktvena bahudhā viśvatomukham
Word by word
Others also, sacrificing with the sacrifice of knowledge, worship me: as one, as distinct, and in many ways as the one who faces everywhere.

Bhāṣya Knowledge of the Lord is itself the sacrifice; they leave every other worship. Some worship with the vision of the highest truth, that the supreme Brahman is one only; some in the thought that the same Lord Viṣṇu exists in distinct forms as the sun, the moon and so on; some in the thought that the same Lord, of universal form, exists in many ways.

अहं क्रतुरहं यज्ञः स्वधाहमहमौषधम् । मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हुतम् ॥ १६ ॥
ahaṃ kratur ahaṃ yajñaḥ svadhāham ahamauṣadham | mantro 'ham ahamevājyam ahamagnir ahaṃ hutam || 16 ||
Padaccheda
अहम् क्रतुः अहम् यज्ञः स्वधा अहम् अहम् औषधम् मन्त्रः अहम् अहम् एव आज्यम् अहम् अग्निः अहम् हुतम्
aham kratuḥ aham yajñaḥ svadhā aham aham auṣadham mantraḥ aham aham eva ājyam aham agniḥ aham hutam
Word by word
I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to the ancestors, I am the plants that are food; I am the sacred formula, I myself am the clarified butter, I am the fire, I am the act of offering.

Bhāṣya This answers how, if they worship in many ways, they worship him alone. kratu is a rite of the Veda, yajña one taught in the law books. svadhā is the food given to the ancestors; auṣadha is what all living beings eat, rice, barley and the like. Or svadhā is food in general and auṣadha medicine.

पिताहमस्य जगतो माता धाता पितामहः । वेद्यं पवित्रमोंकार ऋक्साम यजुरेव च ॥ १७ ॥
pitāhamasya jagato mātā dhātā pitāmahaḥ | vedyaṃ pavitramoṃkāra ṛksāma yajureva ca || 17 ||
Padaccheda
पिता अहम् अस्य जगतः माता धाता पितामहः वेद्यम् पवित्रम् ओंकारः ऋक् साम यजुः एव च
pitā aham asya jagataḥ mātā dhātā pitāmahaḥ vedyam pavitram oṃkāraḥ ṛk sāma yajuḥ eva ca
Word by word
I am the father of this world, the mother, the dispenser, the grandfather; what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable Om, and the Ṛg, the Sāma and the Yajur Veda.

Bhāṣya The dispenser of the fruit of action to living beings.

गतिर्भर्ता प्रभुः साक्षी निवासः शरणं सुहृत् । प्रभवः प्रलयः स्थानं निधानं बीजमव्ययम् ॥ १८ ॥
gatirbhartā prabhuḥ sākṣī nivāsaḥ śaraṇaṃ suhṛt | prabhavaḥ pralayaḥ sthānaṃ nidhānaṃ bījamavyayam || 18 ||
Padaccheda
गतिः भर्ता प्रभुः साक्षी निवासः शरणम् सुहृत् प्रभवः प्रलयः स्थानम् निधानम् बीजम् अव्ययम्
gatiḥ bhartā prabhuḥ sākṣī nivāsaḥ śaraṇam suhṛt prabhavaḥ pralayaḥ sthānam nidhānam bījam avyayam
Word by word
I am the goal, the sustainer, the master, the witness, the dwelling, the refuge, the friend; the origin, the dissolution, the ground, the treasure-store, and the imperishable seed.

Bhāṣya The goal is the fruit of action; the witness of what living beings have done and left undone; the refuge of the distressed; a friend who helps without looking for a return. The treasure-store is what living beings are to enjoy at a later time. The seed is imperishable because it lasts as long as the cycle of birth and death lasts.

तपाम्यहमहं वर्षं निगृह्णाम्युत्सृजामि च । अमृतं चैव मृत्युश्च सदसच्चाहमर्जुन ॥ १९ ॥
tapāmyaham ahaṃ varṣaṃ nigṛhṇāmyutsṛjāmi ca | amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca sadasaccāhamarjuna || 19 ||
Padaccheda
तपामि अहम् अहम् वर्षम् निगृह्णामि उत्सृजामि च अमृतम् च एव मृत्युः च सत् असत् च अहम् अर्जुन
tapāmi aham aham varṣam nigṛhṇāmi utsṛjāmi ca amṛtam ca eva mṛtyuḥ ca sat asat ca aham arjuna
Word by word
I give heat; I hold back and send forth the rain; I am immortality and death, what is and what is not, O Arjuna.

Bhāṣya As the sun he gives heat with some rays, and with others sends rain in the rainy season and holds it back for eight months. Immortality is that of the gods, death that of mortals. sat is what exists in relation to something, asat the opposite; the Lord is not himself utterly non-existent. Or sat and asat are effect and cause.

त्रैविद्या मां सोमपाः पूतपापा यज्ञैरिष्ट्वा स्वर्गतिं प्रार्थयन्ते । ते पुण्यमासाद्य सुरेन्द्रलोकमश्नन्ति दिव्यान्दिवि देवभोगान् ॥ २० ॥
traividyā māṃ somapāḥ pūtapāpā yajñairiṣṭvā svargatiṃ prārthayante | te puṇyamāsādya surendralokamaśnanti divyāndivi devabhogān || 20 ||
Padaccheda
त्रैविद्याः माम् सोम-पाः पूत-पापाः यज्ञैः इष्ट्वा स्वर्-गतिम् प्रार्थयन्ते ते पुण्यम् आसाद्य सुर-इन्द्र-लोकम् अश्नन्ति दिव्यान् दिवि देव-भोगान्
traividyāḥ mām soma-pāḥ pūta-pāpāḥ yajñaiḥ iṣṭvā svar-gatim prārthayante te puṇyam āsādya sura-indra-lokam aśnanti divyān divi deva-bhogān
Word by word
The knowers of the three Vedas, who drink the soma and are cleansed of sin, having worshipped me with sacrifices, pray for the way to heaven. Having reached the holy world of the lord of the gods, they enjoy in heaven the divine pleasures of the gods.

Bhāṣya Those who worship with the knowledge described reach the Lord according to their knowledge; these are the ignorant who desire pleasures. They worship him in the form of the Vasus and other gods, with the Agniṣṭoma and the like. puṇyam he takes as the fruit of merit, which they attain; it is rendered here with lokam.

ते तं भुक्त्वा स्वर्गलोकं विशालं क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्यलोकं विशन्ति । एवं त्रयीधर्ममनुप्रपन्ना गतागतं कामकामा लभन्ते ॥ २१ ॥
te taṃ bhuktvā svargalokaṃ viśālaṃ kṣīṇe puṇye martyalokaṃ viśanti | evaṃ trayīdharmamanuprapannā gatāgataṃ kāmakāmā labhante || 21 ||
Padaccheda
ते तम् भुक्त्वा स्वर्ग-लोकम् विशालम् क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्य-लोकम् विशन्ति एवम् त्रयी-धर्मम् अनुप्रपन्नाः गत-आगतम् काम-कामाः लभन्ते
te tam bhuktvā svarga-lokam viśālam kṣīṇe puṇye martya-lokam viśanti evam trayī-dharmam anuprapannāḥ gata-āgatam kāma-kāmāḥ labhante
Word by word
Having enjoyed that vast world of heaven, they enter the world of mortals when their merit is exhausted. Thus, following the dharma of the three Vedas, those who desire pleasures obtain going and coming.

Bhāṣya Following Vedic ritual alone, they get only going and coming, and nowhere any independence.

अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते । तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम् ॥ २२ ॥
ananyāścintayanto māṃ ye janāḥ paryupāsate | teṣāṃ nityābhiyuktānāṃ yogakṣemaṃ vahāmyaham || 22 ||
Padaccheda
अन्-अन्याः चिन्तयन्तः माम् ये जनाः पर्युपासते तेषाम् नित्य-अभियुक्तानाम् योगक्षेमम् वहामि अहम्
an-anyāḥ cintayantaḥ mām ye janāḥ paryupāsate teṣām nitya-abhiyuktānām yogakṣemam vahāmi aham
Word by word
Those people who worship me, thinking of me as not other than themselves: of those ever-devoted ones I take care of the gain and the safekeeping.

Bhāṣya These are the renouncers, free of desire and of right vision, who have come to the supreme god Nārāyaṇa as their own Self. yoga is gaining what one does not have, kṣema keeping what one has. The Lord does this for other devotees too; but they also strive for it themselves, while those who see no other do not, caring neither for life nor for death, with the Lord as their only refuge.

येऽप्यन्यदेवता भक्ता यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः । तेऽपि मामेव कौन्तेय यजन्त्यविधिपूर्वकम् ॥ २३ ॥
ye 'pyanyadevatā bhaktā yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ | te 'pi māmeva kaunteya yajantyavidhipūrvakam || 23 ||
Padaccheda
ये अपि अन्य-देवता-भक्ताः यजन्ते श्रद्धया अन्विताः ते अपि माम् एव कौन्तेय यजन्ति अविधि-पूर्वकम्
ye api anya-devatā-bhaktāḥ yajante śraddhayā anvitāḥ te api mām eva kaunteya yajanti avidhi-pūrvakam
Word by word
Those also who, devoted to other gods, sacrifice with faith: they too sacrifice to me alone, O son of Kuntī, though in ignorance.

Bhāṣya Avidhi is ignorance; they sacrifice with ignorance going before.

Bhāṣya Śaṅkara reads anyadevatābhaktāḥ as one compound; the DCS divides it into two words.

अहं हि सर्वयज्ञानां भोक्ता च प्रभुरेव च । न तु मामभिजानन्ति तत्त्वेनातश्च्यवन्ति ते ॥ २४ ॥
ahaṃ hi sarvayajñānāṃ bhoktā ca prabhureva ca | na tu māmabhijānanti tattvenātaścyavanti te || 24 ||
Padaccheda
अहम् हि सर्व-यज्ञानाम् भोक्ता च प्रभुः एव च न तु माम् अभिजानन्ति तत्त्वेन अतः च्यवन्ति ते
aham hi sarva-yajñānām bhoktā ca prabhuḥ eva ca na tu mām abhijānanti tattvena ataḥ cyavanti te
Word by word
For I am the enjoyer and the lord of all sacrifices; but they do not know me as I truly am, and therefore they fall.

Bhāṣya Of all sacrifices, Vedic and of the law books; he is their enjoyer as the Self of the deity and their master (8.4). Having sacrificed in ignorance, they fall away from the fruit of the sacrifice.

यान्ति देवव्रता देवान्पितॄन्यान्ति पितृव्रताः । भूतानि यान्ति भूतेज्या यान्ति मद्याजिनोऽपि माम् ॥ २५ ॥
yānti devavratā devān pitṝnyānti pitṛvratāḥ | bhūtāni yānti bhūtejyā yānti madyājino 'pi mām || 25 ||
Padaccheda
यान्ति देव-व्रताः देवान् पितॄन् यान्ति पितृ-व्रताः भूतानि यान्ति भूत-इज्याः यान्ति मद्-याजिनः अपि माम्
yānti deva-vratāḥ devān pitṝn yānti pitṛ-vratāḥ bhūtāni yānti bhūta-ijyāḥ yānti mad-yājinaḥ api mām
Word by word
Those vowed to the gods go to the gods; those vowed to the ancestors go to the ancestors; those who worship the spirits go to the spirits; and those who sacrifice to me go to me.

Bhāṣya Even those who sacrifice in ignorance surely get the fruit of their sacrifice. The spirits are the Vināyakas, the troops of the Mothers, the four Sisters and the like. Those who sacrifice to the Lord are the Vaiṣṇavas. Though the effort is the same, out of ignorance the others do not worship him, and so receive a small fruit.

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति । तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥ २६ ॥
patraṃ puṣpaṃ phalaṃ toyaṃ yo me bhaktyā prayacchati | tadahaṃ bhaktyupahṛtamaśnāmi prayatātmanaḥ || 26 ||
Padaccheda
पत्रम् पुष्पम् फलम् तोयम् यः मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति तत् अहम् भक्ति-उपहृतम् अश्नामि प्रयत-आत्मनः
patram puṣpam phalam toyam yaḥ me bhaktyā prayacchati tat aham bhakti-upahṛtam aśnāmi prayata-ātmanaḥ
Word by word
Whoever offers me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water: that, offered with devotion by the pure-minded one, I accept.

Bhāṣya Not only is the fruit for his devotees endless, the state of no return; he is also easy to worship.

यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यज्जुहोषि ददासि यत् । यत्तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम् ॥ २७ ॥
yatkaroṣi yadaśnāsi yajjuhoṣi dadāsi yat | yattapasyasi kaunteya tatkuruṣva madarpaṇam || 27 ||
Padaccheda
यत् करोषि यत् अश्नासि यत् जुहोषि ददासि यत् यत् तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत् कुरुष्व मद्-अर्पणम्
yat karoṣi yat aśnāsi yat juhoṣi dadāsi yat yat tapasyasi kaunteya tat kuruṣva mad-arpaṇam
Word by word
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give, whatever austerity you practise, O son of Kuntī, do that as an offering to me.

Bhāṣya What you do of your own accord; what you offer, in a rite of the Veda or of the law books; what you give, gold, food, butter and the like, to brahmins and others.

शुभाशुभफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः । संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि ॥ २८ ॥
śubhāśubhaphalairevaṃ mokṣyase karmabandhanaiḥ | saṃnyāsayogayuktātmā vimukto māmupaiṣyasi || 28 ||
Padaccheda
शुभ-अशुभ-फलैः एवम् मोक्ष्यसे कर्म-बन्धनैः संन्यास-योग-युक्त-आत्मा विमुक्तः माम् उपैष्यसि
śubha-aśubha-phalaiḥ evam mokṣyase karma-bandhanaiḥ saṃnyāsa-yoga-yukta-ātmā vimuktaḥ mām upaiṣyasi
Word by word
Thus you will be freed from the bonds of action, with their good and bad fruits; with mind joined to the yoga of renunciation, liberated, you will come to me.

Bhāṣya This is called the yoga of renunciation: it is renunciation because it is an offering to the Lord, and yoga because it is action. He is liberated from the bonds of action while still living, and comes to the Lord when this body falls.

समोऽहं सर्वभूतेषु न मे द्वेष्योऽस्ति न प्रियः । ये भजन्ति तु मां भक्त्या मयि ते तेषु चाप्यहम् ॥ २९ ॥
samo 'haṃ sarvabhūteṣu na me dveṣyo 'sti na priyaḥ | ye bhajanti tu māṃ bhaktyā mayi te teṣu cāpyaham || 29 ||
Padaccheda
समः अहम् सर्व-भूतेषु न मे द्वेष्यः अस्ति न प्रियः ये भजन्ति तु माम् भक्त्या मयि ते तेषु च अपि अहम्
samaḥ aham sarva-bhūteṣu na me dveṣyaḥ asti na priyaḥ ye bhajanti tu mām bhaktyā mayi te teṣu ca api aham
Word by word
I am the same towards all beings; none is hateful to me and none dear. But those who worship me with devotion are in me, and I also am in them.

Bhāṣya This answers the charge that the Lord, favouring his devotees and not others, has attachment and aversion. He is like fire, which removes the cold of those who come near and not of those who stay far off. Those who worship him are in him by the nature of things, not because of any attachment of his, and he is in them in the same way; this does not mean he hates the others.

अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक् । साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः ॥ ३० ॥
api cetsudurācāro bhajate māmananyabhāk | sādhureva sa mantavyaḥ samyagvyavasito hi saḥ || 30 ||
Padaccheda
अपि चेत् सु-दुराचारः भजते माम् अन्-अन्य-भाक् साधुः एव सः मन्तव्यः सम्यक् व्यवसितः हि सः
api cet su-durācāraḥ bhajate mām an-anya-bhāk sādhuḥ eva saḥ mantavyaḥ samyak vyavasitaḥ hi saḥ
Word by word
Even if a man of very evil conduct worships me with devotion to no other, he is to be regarded as good indeed, for he has rightly resolved.

Bhāṣya This is the greatness of devotion. His resolve is a good one.

क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति । कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति ॥ ३१ ॥
kṣipraṃ bhavati dharmātmā śaśvacchāntiṃ nigacchati | kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati || 31 ||
Padaccheda
क्षिप्रम् भवति धर्म-आत्मा शश्वत् शान्तिम् निगच्छति कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति
kṣipram bhavati dharma-ātmā śaśvat śāntim nigacchati kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati
Word by word
Quickly his mind becomes set on dharma and he attains lasting peace. O son of Kuntī, proclaim it for certain: my devotee does not perish.

Bhāṣya He leaves his outward evil conduct through the power of his inner right resolve. The devotee is one who has given his inner self over to the Lord.

मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः । स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम् ॥ ३२ ॥
māṃ hi pārtha vyapāśritya ye 'pi syuḥ pāpayonayaḥ | striyo vaiśyāstathā śūdrāste 'pi yānti parāṃ gatim || 32 ||
Padaccheda
माम् हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य ये अपि स्युः पाप-योनयः स्त्रियः वैश्याः तथा शूद्राः ते अपि यान्ति पराम् गतिम्
mām hi pārtha vyapāśritya ye api syuḥ pāpa-yonayaḥ striyaḥ vaiśyāḥ tathā śūdrāḥ te api yānti parām gatim
Word by word
For taking refuge in me, O Pārtha, even those who may be of low birth, women, traders and servants, they too go to the supreme goal.

Bhāṣya Pāpayonayaḥ, those whose birth is low; in answer to ‘who are they?’ he names women, vaiśyas and śūdras.

किं पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा । अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम् ॥ ३३ ॥
kiṃ punarbrāhmaṇāḥ puṇyā bhaktā rājarṣayastathā | anityamasukhaṃ lokamimaṃ prāpya bhajasva mām || 33 ||
Padaccheda
किम् पुनः ब्राह्मणाः पुण्याः भक्ताः राजर्षयः तथा अनित्यम् असुखम् लोकम् इमम् प्राप्य भजस्व माम्
kim punaḥ brāhmaṇāḥ puṇyāḥ bhaktāḥ rājarṣayaḥ tathā anityam asukham lokam imam prāpya bhajasva mām
Word by word
How much more, then, holy brahmins and devoted royal sages. Having come to this impermanent, joyless world, worship me.

Bhāṣya Holy means of holy birth. This world is the world of men: having gained human birth, which is hard to gain and is the means to the ends of life.

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु । मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥ ३४ ॥
manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru | māmevaiṣyasi yuktvaivamātmānaṃ matparāyaṇaḥ || 34 ||
Padaccheda
मद्-मनाः भव मद्-भक्तः मद्-याजी माम् नमस्कुरु माम् एव एष्यसि युक्त्वा एवम् आत्मानम् मद्-परायणः
mad-manāḥ bhava mad-bhaktaḥ mad-yājī mām namaskuru mām eva eṣyasi yuktvā evam ātmānam mad-parāyaṇaḥ
Word by word
Be with your mind on me, devoted to me, sacrificing to me; bow to me. Having thus collected your mind, with me as your final goal, you will come to me alone, the Self.

Bhāṣya ‘me’ is Vāsudeva. He takes yuktvā as ‘having collected the mind’, and ātmānam with mām: ‘I am the Self of all beings and the supreme goal; to me, who am such, you will come’.
